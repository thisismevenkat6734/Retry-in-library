Railway Recruitment Examinations

English

EXAM OVERVIEW

Understand Each Recruitment Category

RRB NTPC

RRB NTPC recruitment covers notified non-technical railway posts. Different posts may have different educational qualifications and additional skill or aptitude requirements.

Broad preparation areas

  • Mathematics
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Awareness
  • Post-specific aptitude or skill tests where applicable

RRB Group D

Group D recruitment relates to notified Level 1 railway positions. The recruitment process may include a computer-based test, physical efficiency test and document verification.

Broad preparation areas

  • Mathematics
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Science
  • General Awareness and Current Affairs
  • Physical preparation where required

RRB Assistant Loco Pilot — ALP

ALP recruitment has technical eligibility and selection requirements. Candidates should verify the accepted qualifications, medical standards and examination stages in the relevant notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • Mathematics
  • Reasoning and Mental Ability
  • Basic Science and Engineering
  • Relevant technical trade subjects
  • Computer-based aptitude test where prescribed

RRB Technician

Technician recruitment covers notified technical categories. Required qualifications and trade subjects depend on the post and notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • Mathematics
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Science
  • General Awareness
  • Technical and trade-related knowledge

RPF Constable

RPF Constable recruitment is conducted under the applicable Railway Protection Force rules. Selection may include a computer-based test, physical tests and medical examination.

Broad preparation areas

  • Arithmetic
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Awareness
  • Physical fitness preparation

RPF Sub-Inspector

RPF Sub-Inspector recruitment has its own educational, physical and medical requirements. Review the relevant notification before preparing.

Broad preparation areas

  • Arithmetic
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Awareness
  • Physical and medical standards
Important These are broad preparation topics, not a verified complete syllabus. Exam stages, trade requirements, physical standards and marks may change by notification.
CORE SUBJECTS

Important Railway Exam Subjects

➗

Mathematics

Number systems, percentages, ratios, averages, time and work, speed and distance.

🧠

Reasoning

Analogies, series, coding-decoding, puzzles and analytical reasoning.

🔬

General Science

Fundamental concepts in Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

🌍

General Awareness

History, geography, polity, economy and important national developments.

📰

Current Affairs

Important events, sports, awards, science developments and government initiatives.

⚙️

Technical Knowledge

Trade-specific concepts for relevant technical railway recruitment posts.

YOUR STUDY JOURNEY

How to Prepare for Railway Exams

Step 1 — Identify the correct post

Check educational qualifications, age limits, medical standards and post requirements.

Step 2 — Study mathematics daily

Practise calculations and improve accuracy through topic-wise exercises.

Step 3 — Improve reasoning speed

Solve different reasoning question types and review your mistakes.

Step 4 — Revise science and awareness

Study fundamental science concepts and regularly revise general awareness.

Step 5 — Take practice tests

Practise timed examinations and analyse your performance after every test.
