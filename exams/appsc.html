ABOUT APPSC

What is APPSC?

English

Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) is the state public service commission responsible for conducting recruitment processes for various government posts in Andhra Pradesh, according to applicable recruitment rules and official notifications.

తెలుగు

ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్ పబ్లిక్ సర్వీస్ కమిషన్ (APPSC) అనేది ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్ రాష్ట్రంలో వివిధ ప్రభుత్వ ఉద్యోగాల నియామక ప్రక్రియలను సంబంధిత నియమాలు మరియు అధికారిక నోటిఫికేషన్ల ప్రకారం నిర్వహించే సంస్థ.

EXAM OVERVIEW

Understand Each Group

APPSC Group 1

Group 1 recruitment is associated with higher-level administrative and state civil service positions. Candidates should study the official notification, prescribed syllabus, examination scheme and eligibility requirements for the specific recruitment cycle.

Preparation areas

  • Indian History and Andhra Pradesh History
  • Indian Constitution and Governance
  • Indian and Andhra Pradesh Economy
  • Indian and Andhra Pradesh Geography
  • Science, Technology and Environment
  • Current Affairs and General Studies
  • Analytical and other notification-specific topics

APPSC Group 2

Group 2 recruitment covers different executive and non-executive positions depending on the recruitment notification. Preparation should be based on the prescribed syllabus and selection procedure for that particular notification.

Preparation areas

  • Indian History and Culture
  • Indian Constitution and Polity
  • Indian and Andhra Pradesh Economy
  • Geography
  • Science and Technology
  • Logical Reasoning and Mental Ability
  • Current Affairs

APPSC Group 3

Group 3 recruitment details, post availability and examination requirements must be checked in the corresponding official notification. Study the prescribed subjects rather than relying on an assumed common examination pattern.

Preparation areas

  • General Studies
  • Indian History and Culture
  • Andhra Pradesh History and Society
  • Indian Constitution
  • Economy and Geography
  • Science and Current Affairs
  • Notification-specific subjects

APPSC Group 4

Group 4 recruitment generally relates to various entry-level government positions. The exact posts, educational qualifications, selection stages and examination syllabus depend on the official notification.

Preparation areas

  • General Knowledge
  • General Science
  • History and Geography
  • Indian Constitution
  • Arithmetic
  • Reasoning and Mental Ability
  • Language and other prescribed topics
Notification-specific syllabus The topics above are broad preparation areas, not a verified, complete official syllabus for every APPSC recruitment. Check the relevant notification before finalising your study plan.

YOUR STUDY JOURNEY

How to Prepare for APPSC

Follow a consistent learning and revision routine.

Step 1 — Understand the notification

Identify the target post, eligibility, syllabus, examination pattern and selection stages from the relevant official notification.

Step 2 — Build subject fundamentals

Start with basic concepts. Study one topic at a time and maintain short revision notes.

Step 3 — Practise MCQs

Solve topic-wise questions and understand why each answer is correct or incorrect.

Step 4 — Revise regularly

Revise important facts, concepts, dates, definitions and previously studied topics.

Step 5 — Take mock tests

Practise under timed conditions and analyse mistakes to improve your preparation.

OFFICIAL INFORMATION

Verify Before You Prepare

Recruitment dates, vacancies, qualifications, age limits, examination patterns and syllabus details can change. Use official APPSC announcements as the final authority.
