ABOUT UPSC

What is UPSC Civil Services Examination?

English

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the Civil Services Examination for recruitment to various services under the Government of India. The examination includes multiple stages and tests a candidate's knowledge, analytical ability, judgement and suitability for public service.

The Civil Services Examination provides access to different services according to the applicable recruitment rules, preferences and merit.

Administrative responsibilities at district, state and central government levels.

EXAM STRUCTURE

Three Stages of UPSC CSE

Stage 1 — Preliminary Examination

The Preliminary Examination is the screening stage of the Civil Services Examination.

General Studies Paper I

  • Current events of national and international importance
  • History of India and Indian National Movement
  • Indian and World Geography
  • Indian Polity and Governance
  • Economic and Social Development
  • Environment, Ecology and Biodiversity
  • General Science

General Studies Paper II — CSAT

  • Comprehension
  • Interpersonal and communication skills
  • Logical reasoning and analytical ability
  • Decision-making and problem-solving
  • General mental ability
  • Basic numeracy and data interpretation
CSAT is qualifying in nature under the applicable examination scheme. Verify the current notification for the qualifying standard and paper rules.

Stage 2 — Main Examination

The Main Examination evaluates a candidate's understanding, analytical ability, writing skills and knowledge of relevant subjects.

Qualifying language papers

  • Paper A — One Indian language
  • Paper B — English

Merit papers

  • Essay
  • General Studies Paper I
  • General Studies Paper II
  • General Studies Paper III
  • General Studies Paper IV
  • Optional Subject Paper I
  • Optional Subject Paper II

The exact paper structure, exemptions and marks must be checked in the relevant annual notification.

Stage 3 — Personality Test

The Personality Test assesses the candidate's suitability for a career in public service. It may evaluate awareness, clarity of thought, judgement, communication, integrity and balance.

Preparation areas

  • Detailed Application Form awareness
  • Current national and international developments
  • Home state and district awareness
  • Educational and professional background
  • Ethical judgement and balanced opinions
  • Clear and honest communication

OPTIONAL SUBJECT

Choose Your Optional Carefully

The UPSC Main Examination includes two optional subject papers. Candidates should select an optional based on their interest, understanding, syllabus, available resources and ability to write answers.

Examples of optional subjects

  • History
  • Geography
  • Political Science and International Relations
  • Public Administration
  • Sociology
  • Anthropology
  • Economics
  • Philosophy
  • Psychology
  • Mathematics
  • Telugu Literature
  • English Literature

This is an illustrative list, not the complete official optional-subject list.

YOUR STUDY JOURNEY

How to Prepare for UPSC

Step 1 — Read the official syllabus

Understand the Prelims and Mains syllabus before choosing books or study resources.

Step 2 — Build basic concepts

Study history, geography, polity, economy, science and other fundamental areas.

Step 3 — Follow current affairs

Connect current developments with static subjects and maintain reliable notes.

Step 4 — Practise answer writing

Develop structured answers with clear introductions, analysis and conclusions.

Step 5 — Take tests and revise

Analyse your mistakes, practise timed papers and revise consistently.
