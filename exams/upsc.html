ABOUT UPSC

What is UPSC Civil Services Examination?

English

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the Civil Services Examination for recruitment to various services under the Government of India. The examination includes multiple stages and tests a candidate's knowledge, analytical ability, judgement and suitability for public service.

తెలుగు

యూనియన్ పబ్లిక్ సర్వీస్ కమిషన్ (UPSC) భారత ప్రభుత్వంలోని వివిధ సివిల్ సర్వీసులకు అభ్యర్థులను ఎంపిక చేయడానికి సివిల్ సర్వీసెస్ పరీక్షను నిర్వహిస్తుంది. ఈ పరీక్షలో అభ్యర్థుల జ్ఞానం, విశ్లేషణ సామర్థ్యం, నిర్ణయ సామర్థ్యం మరియు ప్రజాసేవకు తగిన నైపుణ్యాలను పరిశీలిస్తారు.

EXAM STRUCTURE

Three Stages of UPSC CSE

Stage 1 — Preliminary Examination

The Preliminary Examination is the screening stage of the Civil Services Examination.

General Studies Paper I

  • Current events of national and international importance
  • History of India and Indian National Movement
  • Indian and World Geography
  • Indian Polity and Governance
  • Economic and Social Development
  • Environment, Ecology and Biodiversity
  • General Science

General Studies Paper II — CSAT

  • Comprehension
  • Interpersonal and communication skills
  • Logical reasoning and analytical ability
  • Decision-making and problem-solving
  • General mental ability
  • Basic numeracy and data interpretation
CSAT is qualifying in nature under the applicable examination scheme. Verify the current notification for the qualifying standard and paper rules.

Stage 2 — Main Examination

The Main Examination evaluates a candidate's understanding, analytical ability, writing skills and knowledge of relevant subjects.

Qualifying language papers

  • Paper A — One Indian language
  • Paper B — English

Merit papers

  • Essay
  • General Studies Paper I
  • General Studies Paper II
  • General Studies Paper III
  • General Studies Paper IV
  • Optional Subject Paper I
  • Optional Subject Paper II

The exact paper structure, exemptions and marks must be checked in the relevant annual notification.

Stage 3 — Personality Test

The Personality Test assesses the candidate's suitability for a career in public service. It may evaluate awareness, clarity of thought, judgement, communication, integrity and balance.

Preparation areas

  • Detailed Application Form awareness
  • Current national and international developments
  • Home state and district awareness
  • Educational and professional background
  • Ethical judgement and balanced opinions
  • Clear and honest communication

UPSC CSE — Detailed Syllabus & Study Areas

Prelims — General Studies Paper I

English
  1. Current events: national and international developments, government policies, reports, awards, important institutions and places in the news.
  2. History: ancient, medieval and modern Indian history; cultural heritage; the freedom struggle and the national movement.
  3. Geography: physical geography, Indian and world geography, climate, rivers, resources, agriculture, population and economic geography.
  4. Polity and governance: Constitution, its features, Parliament, Executive, Judiciary, federalism, local government, constitutional and statutory bodies, public policy and rights issues.
  5. Economy and social development: growth, inflation, budgeting, banking, poverty, inclusion, demographics, employment, sustainable development and social-sector initiatives.
  6. Environment: ecology, ecosystems, biodiversity, protected areas, pollution, conservation and climate change.
  7. General Science: everyday science, basic biology, physics, chemistry, health, space, biotechnology and science developments.
తెలుగు
  1. కరెంట్ అఫైర్స్: జాతీయ, అంతర్జాతీయ పరిణామాలు, ప్రభుత్వ విధానాలు, నివేదికలు, అవార్డులు, ముఖ్య సంస్థలు.
  2. చరిత్ర: ప్రాచీన, మధ్యయుగ, ఆధునిక భారత చరిత్ర, సంస్కృతి, స్వాతంత్ర్య ఉద్యమం.
  3. భూగోళశాస్త్రం: భౌతిక భూగోళం, భారతదేశం మరియు ప్రపంచ భూగోళం, వాతావరణం, నదులు, వనరులు, వ్యవసాయం, జనాభా.

Prelims — General Studies Paper II (CSAT)

English
  • Reading comprehension and understanding passages.
  • Interpersonal skills and communication.
  • Logical reasoning, analytical ability and problem solving.
  • Decision making and general mental ability.
  • Basic numeracy: numbers, ratios, percentages, averages, profit and loss, time and work, speed and distance.
  • Data interpretation: tables, charts, graphs and data sufficiency.
తెలుగు
  • పఠన అవగాహన, ఇచ్చిన పేరాలను అర్థం చేసుకోవడం.
  • వ్యక్తుల మధ్య సంబంధ నైపుణ్యాలు, కమ్యూనికేషన్.
  • తార్కిక ఆలోచన, విశ్లేషణ సామర్థ్యం, సమస్య పరిష్కారం.
  • నిర్ణయ సామర్థ్యం, సాధారణ మానసిక సామర్థ్యం.
  • ప్రాథమిక గణితం: సంఖ్యలు, నిష్పత్తులు, శాతాలు, సగటులు, లాభనష్టాలు, కాలం-పని, వేగం-దూరం.
  • డేటా విశ్లేషణ: పట్టికలు, చార్టులు, గ్రాఫ్‌లు.

In the 2026 CSE notice, CSAT is a 200-mark objective paper and qualifying at 33%. Candidates must appear in both Prelims papers. Confirm the rules in the notification for the year you apply.

Mains — General Studies Papers I to IV

English
  • GS I — Indian Heritage, History, Geography and Society: art and culture; modern Indian history; freedom struggle; post-independence consolidation; world history; Indian society; women, population, poverty, urbanisation, globalisation; physical geography and geographical phenomena.
  • GS II — Constitution, Governance, Social Justice and International Relations: Constitution and amendments; federalism; Parliament and State Legislatures; Executive and Judiciary; constitutional/statutory bodies; government policies; welfare schemes; health, education and human resources; transparency, accountability and e-governance; India and the world.
  • GS III — Economy, Technology, Environment, Security and Disaster Management: Indian economy, planning, mobilisation of resources, growth and employment; inclusive growth; budgeting; agriculture; food processing; infrastructure; science and technology; environment and biodiversity; disaster management; internal security and cyber security.
  • GS IV — Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude: ethics and human values; attitude; aptitude and foundational values for civil service; emotional intelligence; moral thinkers; public-service ethics; probity in governance; codes of conduct; corruption; and case studies.
తెలుగు
  • GS I: భారతీయ కళలు, సంస్కృతి, ఆధునిక భారత చరిత్ర, స్వాతంత్ర్య పోరాటం, ప్రపంచ చరిత్ర, భారత సమాజం, మహిళలు, జనాభా, పేదరికం, పట్టణీకరణ, భౌతిక భూగోళశాస్త్రం.
  • GS II: రాజ్యాంగం, సవరణలు, సమాఖ్య విధానం, పార్లమెంట్, రాష్ట్ర శాసనసభలు, కార్యనిర్వాహక వ్యవస్థ, న్యాయవ్యవస్థ, ప్రభుత్వ విధానాలు, సంక్షేమ పథకాలు, ఆరోగ్యం, విద్య, పారదర్శకత, అంతర్జాతీయ సంబంధాలు.
  • GS III: భారత ఆర్థిక వ్యవస్థ, ప్రణాళిక, వనరుల సమీకరణ, వృద్ధి, ఉపాధి, వ్యవసాయం, మౌలిక సదుపాయాలు, విజ్ఞాన సాంకేతికత, పర్యావరణం, విపత్తు నిర్వహణ, అంతర్గత భద్రత.
  • GS IV: నైతికత, మానవ విలువలు, వైఖరి, సివిల్ సర్వీస్ విలువలు, భావోద్వేగ మేధస్సు, ప్రజాసేవ నైతికత, పాలనలో నిజాయితీ, ప్రవర్తనా నియమాలు, కేస్ స్టడీలు.

Mains — Essay, Qualifying Papers and Optional

  • Essay: practise organising ideas, building a clear argument, using relevant examples and writing a balanced conclusion.
  • Qualifying language papers: Indian Language and English. Check exemptions and qualifying standards in the current rules.
  • Optional subject: two papers from one subject listed in the official notification. Read the complete syllabus before selecting it.
  • Answer writing: answer the exact demand, use headings where helpful, support points with examples and stay within the word limit.

  • ఎస్సే: ఆలోచనలను క్రమబద్ధీకరించడం, స్పష్టమైన వాదన, సరైన ఉదాహరణలు, సమతుల్య ముగింపు సాధన చేయాలి.

PRACTICAL STUDY METHOD

Daily & Weekly UPSC Study Routine

Daily routine — adaptable example

  • Read one newspaper or trusted current-affairs source and make short issue-based notes.
  • Study one core subject from the syllabus and revise the previous day's topic.
  • Practise a small set of Prelims MCQs and analyse every wrong answer.
  • Write one Mains answer regularly; focus on structure and relevance, not decorative language.
  • Practise CSAT consistently, especially if comprehension or mathematics needs improvement.

This is a sample routine, not a mandatory timetable. Adjust study hours to your responsibilities and learning pace.

Weekly revision cycle

  • Revise the topics studied during the week.
  • Attempt a timed topic-wise test.
  • Maintain an error notebook: concept gap, factual error, misreading or time-management issue.
  • Practise one essay outline or a set of Mains answers.
  • Update current-affairs notes by linking issues to the syllabus.

How to make useful notes

  • Keep each note connected to a syllabus heading.
  • Separate facts, causes, impacts, challenges and solutions.
  • Use maps, timelines, flowcharts and examples where they improve understanding.
  • Record the source and date for changing facts and statistics.
  • Revise and shorten notes instead of collecting unlimited material.
OFFICIAL SCHEME REFERENCE

Important Exam Scheme — 2026 Notice

According to the UPSC Civil Services Examination 2026 notice:

  • Prelims has two objective papers of 200 marks each. It is a screening stage; Prelims marks are not counted in final merit.
  • CSAT (Paper II) is qualifying, with a 33% minimum qualifying standard stated in the notice.
  • Mains written examination consists of nine conventional/essay-type papers, including two qualifying language papers.
  • The seven merit papers are Essay, four General Studies papers and two Optional Subject papers.
  • The Personality Test carries 275 marks under the 2026 scheme.
