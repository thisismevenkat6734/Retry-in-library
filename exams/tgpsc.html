ABOUT TGPSC

What is TGPSC?

English

Telangana Public Service Commission (TGPSC) is the constitutional recruitment body responsible for conducting recruitment processes for various Telangana state government services and posts under applicable rules and official notifications.

తెలుగు

తెలంగాణ పబ్లిక్ సర్వీస్ కమిషన్ (TGPSC) అనేది సంబంధిత నియమాలు మరియు అధికారిక నోటిఫికేషన్ల ప్రకారం తెలంగాణ రాష్ట్ర ప్రభుత్వంలోని వివిధ సేవలు మరియు ఉద్యోగాల నియామక ప్రక్రియలను నిర్వహించే రాజ్యాంగబద్ధమైన సంస్థ.

EXAM OVERVIEW

Understand Each Group

TGPSC Group 1

Group 1 recruitment is associated with higher-level administrative positions. The examination stages, eligibility and syllabus must be confirmed from the relevant recruitment notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • Indian History and Telangana History
  • Indian Constitution and Governance
  • Indian Economy and Telangana Economy
  • Indian and Telangana Geography
  • Science, Technology and Environment
  • Telangana Movement and State Formation
  • Current Affairs and General Studies

TGPSC Group 2

Group 2 recruitment may include different executive and non-executive posts. Always use the relevant notification to identify the prescribed papers and selection process.

Broad preparation areas

  • History and Cultural Heritage of Telangana
  • Indian Constitution and Polity
  • Social and Economic Development
  • Telangana Economy
  • Science and Technology
  • Logical Reasoning and Mental Ability
  • Current Affairs

TGPSC Group 3

Group 3 recruitment requirements depend on the corresponding notification. Candidates should review the official paper structure and prescribed subjects.

Broad preparation areas

  • General Studies and General Abilities
  • History and Cultural Heritage of Telangana
  • Indian Constitution and Governance
  • Economy and Development
  • Telangana Movement and State Formation
  • Science and Environment
  • Current Affairs

TGPSC Group 4

Group 4 recruitment generally relates to various entry-level government posts. The exact selection procedure and syllabus depend on the notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • General Knowledge
  • History and Geography
  • Indian Constitution
  • Telangana History and Culture
  • Arithmetic and Numerical Ability
  • Reasoning and Mental Ability
  • General Science and Current Affairs
Notification-specific syllabus The lists above are broad study guidance, not a verified complete official syllabus for every recruitment cycle. Confirm all topics against the applicable official notification.

DETAILED STUDY CONTENT

Telangana Focus Areas – Topic-wise Study Roadmap

Use these topic headings to organise notes and revision. Match the final list with the syllabus in the relevant recruitment notification.

1. Telangana History and Heritage

  • Early historical background and important archaeological and inscriptional evidence.
  • Major dynasties associated with the region: Satavahanas, Vakatakas, Chalukyas, Kakatiyas, Delhi Sultanate influences, Bahmani and Qutb Shahi periods, and Asaf Jahi rule.
  • Kakatiya administration, irrigation works, temple architecture and the significance of Warangal.
  • Hyderabad State under the Nizams; social, cultural and administrative developments.
  • People's movements, peasant struggles and the Telangana armed struggle: causes, participants, major developments and consequences.

తెలుగు: తెలంగాణ ప్రాచీన చరిత్ర, శాసనాలు, ముఖ్య రాజవంశాలు, కాకతీయుల పాలన, కోటలు–దేవాలయాలు, నిజాం పాలన, ప్రజా ఉద్యమాలు మరియు తెలంగాణ సాయుధ పోరాటం వంటి అంశాలను కాలక్రమంలో చదవండి.

2. Telangana Movement and State Formation

  • Historical background of the demand for a separate Telangana state.
  • Formation of Andhra State and the 1956 reorganisation context.
  • Gentlemen's Agreement and safeguards: understand the historical context and purpose.
  • 1969 Telangana movement: background, student participation, leadership and outcomes.
  • Developments from the 1990s onward, formation of political platforms and key phases of the movement.
  • Parliamentary process and the legal formation of Telangana in 2014.

తెలుగు: ప్రత్యేక తెలంగాణ డిమాండ్‌కు కారణాలు, 1956 విలీనం, పెద్దమనుషుల ఒప్పందం, 1969 ఉద్యమం, తదనంతర దశలు, 2014 రాష్ట్ర ఆవిర్భావ ప్రక్రియను సంఘటనల క్రమంలో అధ్యయనం చేయండి.

3. Telangana Geography

  • Location, boundaries, physiographic regions and major landforms.
  • Godavari and Krishna river systems; important tributaries and basin concepts.
  • Climate, rainfall patterns, soils, forests and biodiversity.
  • Major irrigation and water-resource projects; learn location, river and purpose from reliable maps and official sources.
  • Districts, urban centres, transport links and resource distribution.

తెలుగు: రాష్ట్ర స్థానం, భౌతిక స్వరూపం, గోదావరి–కృష్ణా నదీ వ్యవస్థలు, వర్షపాతం, నేలలు, అడవులు, నీటిపారుదల ప్రాజెక్టులు మరియు జిల్లాలను మ్యాప్ సహాయంతో చదవండి.

4. Telangana Economy and Development

  • Agriculture, major crops, irrigation, allied activities and rural livelihoods.
  • Industry, information technology, services, employment and urban development.
  • State budget: receipts, expenditure, revenue and fiscal terms.
  • Human development, education, health, welfare and social-sector programmes.
  • Use the latest Telangana Socio Economic Outlook, budget documents and department reports for current figures.

తెలుగు: వ్యవసాయం, పరిశ్రమలు, ఐటీ, సేవారంగం, ఉపాధి, రాష్ట్ర బడ్జెట్, సంక్షేమం మరియు మానవ అభివృద్ధి అంశాలను తాజా ప్రభుత్వ నివేదికల ఆధారంగా చదవండి.

5. Indian Constitution, Governance and Public Administration

  • Constitutional framework, Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties.
  • Union and State executive, legislature and judiciary; federal relations.
  • Local self-government, constitutional and statutory bodies.
  • Public policy, transparency, accountability, citizen services and e-governance.
  • State administration and the role of district-level institutions.

తెలుగు: రాజ్యాంగ పీఠిక, ప్రాథమిక హక్కులు, ఆదేశిక సూత్రాలు, కేంద్ర–రాష్ట్ర సంబంధాలు, స్థానిక స్వపరిపాలన, పరిపాలనా పారదర్శకత వంటి అంశాలను ఉదాహరణలతో నేర్చుకోండి.

6. General Studies, Science and Environment

  • Everyday applications of physics, chemistry and biology.
  • Public health, nutrition, communicable diseases and basic scientific developments.
  • Ecology, ecosystems, biodiversity, protected areas and conservation.
  • Climate change, pollution, disaster management and sustainable development.
  • Information technology, space, biotechnology and emerging technologies at an examination-appropriate level.

తెలుగు: రోజువారీ జీవితంలోని శాస్త్ర అనువర్తనాలు, ఆరోగ్యం, పర్యావరణ వ్యవస్థలు, జీవవైవిధ్యం, కాలుష్యం, విపత్తు నిర్వహణ మరియు సాంకేతిక పరిణామాలను ప్రాథమిక భావనలతో చదవండి.

PRACTICAL STUDY PLAN

Weekly TGPSC Study Cycle

Repeat this cycle and adjust the time allocation to the post and official syllabus you are preparing for.

  • Monday: Telangana history and movement – concept study plus timeline revision.
  • Tuesday: Indian polity and governance – constitutional concepts and short notes.
  • Wednesday: Telangana and Indian geography – maps, rivers, climate and resources.
  • Thursday: Economy – basic concepts, Telangana development and budget terminology.
  • Friday: General science, environment and technology.
  • Saturday: Current affairs and topic-wise MCQs; review every incorrect answer.
  • Sunday: Weekly revision, timed practice and analysis of weak areas.

తెలుగు సూచన: ప్రతి రోజు చదివిన అంశాన్ని అదే రోజు 10–15 నిమిషాలు పునశ్చరణ చేయండి. వారాంతంలో తప్పుగా వచ్చిన ప్రశ్నలకు ప్రత్యేక నోట్ తయారు చేయండి.
