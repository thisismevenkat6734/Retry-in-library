ABOUT TGPSC

What is TGPSC?

English

Telangana Public Service Commission (TGPSC) is the constitutional recruitment body responsible for conducting recruitment processes for various Telangana state government services and posts under applicable rules and official notifications.

తెలుగు

తెలంగాణ పబ్లిక్ సర్వీస్ కమిషన్ (TGPSC) అనేది సంబంధిత నియమాలు మరియు అధికారిక నోటిఫికేషన్ల ప్రకారం తెలంగాణ రాష్ట్ర ప్రభుత్వంలోని వివిధ సేవలు మరియు ఉద్యోగాల నియామక ప్రక్రియలను నిర్వహించే రాజ్యాంగబద్ధమైన సంస్థ.

EXAM OVERVIEW

Understand Each Group

TGPSC Group 1

Group 1 recruitment is associated with higher-level administrative positions. The examination stages, eligibility and syllabus must be confirmed from the relevant recruitment notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • Indian History and Telangana History
  • Indian Constitution and Governance
  • Indian Economy and Telangana Economy
  • Indian and Telangana Geography
  • Science, Technology and Environment
  • Telangana Movement and State Formation
  • Current Affairs and General Studies

TGPSC Group 2

Group 2 recruitment may include different executive and non-executive posts. Always use the relevant notification to identify the prescribed papers and selection process.

Broad preparation areas

  • History and Cultural Heritage of Telangana
  • Indian Constitution and Polity
  • Social and Economic Development
  • Telangana Economy
  • Science and Technology
  • Logical Reasoning and Mental Ability
  • Current Affairs

TGPSC Group 3

Group 3 recruitment requirements depend on the corresponding notification. Candidates should review the official paper structure and prescribed subjects.

Broad preparation areas

  • General Studies and General Abilities
  • History and Cultural Heritage of Telangana
  • Indian Constitution and Governance
  • Economy and Development
  • Telangana Movement and State Formation
  • Science and Environment
  • Current Affairs

TGPSC Group 4

Group 4 recruitment generally relates to various entry-level government posts. The exact selection procedure and syllabus depend on the notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • General Knowledge
  • History and Geography
  • Indian Constitution
  • Telangana History and Culture
  • Arithmetic and Numerical Ability
  • Reasoning and Mental Ability
  • General Science and Current Affairs
Notification-specific syllabus The lists above are broad study guidance, not a verified complete official syllabus for every recruitment cycle. Confirm all topics against the applicable official notification.

YOUR STUDY JOURNEY

How to Prepare for TGPSC

Step 1 — Understand the notification

Verify eligibility, syllabus, examination scheme and selection stages.

Step 2 — Study Telangana-specific subjects

Build a strong foundation in Telangana history, movement, culture, geography and economy.

Step 3 — Learn core subjects

Study history, polity, economy, geography, science and reasoning.

Step 4 — Practise questions

Solve topic-wise MCQs and understand the explanations.

Step 5 — Revise and take tests

Review mistakes, revise important topics and practise timed tests.
