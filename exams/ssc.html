What is the Staff Selection Commission?

English

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts various recruitment examinations for different posts in ministries, departments and organisations of the Government of India, according to applicable rules and recruitment notifications.

EXAM OVERVIEW

Understand the Examinations

SSC Combined Graduate Level — CGL

SSC CGL is conducted for various graduate-level recruitment posts. Educational qualification, examination stages, skill tests and post-specific requirements depend on the notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • English Comprehension
  • General Awareness
  • Computer Knowledge where prescribed
  • Data Entry or other applicable skill tests

SSC Combined Higher Secondary Level — CHSL

SSC CHSL is a recruitment examination for notified posts for which the prescribed educational qualification is generally at the higher-secondary level.

Broad preparation areas

  • English Language
  • General Intelligence
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • General Awareness
  • Skill or typing tests where prescribed

SSC Multi-Tasking Staff — MTS

SSC MTS recruitment covers notified Multi-Tasking Staff posts. Eligibility and selection requirements must be checked for each recruitment cycle.

Broad preparation areas

  • Numerical and Mathematical Ability
  • Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving
  • General Awareness
  • English Language and Comprehension

SSC GD Constable

SSC GD recruitment is conducted for notified constable positions. The selection process may include computer-based examination, physical tests and medical examination.

Broad preparation areas

  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Knowledge and General Awareness
  • Elementary Mathematics
  • English or Hindi as prescribed
  • Physical standards and physical efficiency preparation

SSC Central Police Organisation — CPO

SSC CPO recruitment is associated with notified Sub-Inspector positions. Candidates should check the examination and physical requirements in the applicable notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Knowledge and General Awareness
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • English Comprehension
  • Physical tests and medical standards where applicable

SSC Selection Post

Selection Post recruitment covers different vacancies and qualification levels. The subject requirements vary according to the notified post.

Preparation approach

  • Review the qualification level
  • Identify the prescribed examination subjects
  • Study the relevant General Awareness topics
  • Practise subject-specific questions
  • Check any additional post requirements
Important These are broad preparation areas. Do not treat them as the exact current exam pattern. Paper structure, marks, negative marking, eligibility and selection stages must be verified using the relevant SSC notification.

Important SSC Preparation Subjects

Quantitative Aptitude

🧠

Reasoning

Series, analogy, classification, coding-decoding, puzzles and logical reasoning.

📖

English

Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, sentence improvement and usage.

🌍

General Awareness

History, geography, polity, economy, science and current affairs.

💻

Computer Knowledge

Basic computer concepts and digital awareness where prescribed in the examination.

📰

Current Affairs

National and international events, awards, sports and important developments.

YOUR STUDY JOURNEY

How to Prepare for SSC Exams

Step 1 — Choose the correct examination

Compare the notified posts, qualifications, age requirements and examination scheme.

Step 2 — Strengthen mathematics

Practise calculations and learn methods to solve numerical questions accurately.

Step 3 — Improve reasoning

Practise different reasoning question types and understand the underlying patterns.

Step 4 — Improve English

Study grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension and sentence structure.

Step 5 — Take timed practice tests

Analyse mistakes, improve speed and revise weak topics regularly.
