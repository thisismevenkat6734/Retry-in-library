ABOUT SSC

What is the Staff Selection Commission?

English

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts various recruitment examinations for different posts in ministries, departments and organisations of the Government of India, according to applicable rules and recruitment notifications.

తెలుగు

స్టాఫ్ సెలెక్షన్ కమిషన్ (SSC) భారత ప్రభుత్వంలోని వివిధ మంత్రిత్వ శాఖలు, విభాగాలు మరియు సంస్థల్లోని ఉద్యోగాల కోసం సంబంధిత నియమాలు మరియు నియామక నోటిఫికేషన్ల ప్రకారం పరీక్షలను నిర్వహిస్తుంది.

EXAM OVERVIEW

Understand the Examinations

SSC Combined Graduate Level — CGL

SSC CGL is conducted for various graduate-level recruitment posts. Educational qualification, examination stages, skill tests and post-specific requirements depend on the notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • English Comprehension
  • General Awareness
  • Computer Knowledge where prescribed
  • Data Entry or other applicable skill tests

SSC Combined Higher Secondary Level — CHSL

SSC CHSL is a recruitment examination for notified posts for which the prescribed educational qualification is generally at the higher-secondary level.

Broad preparation areas

  • English Language
  • General Intelligence
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • General Awareness
  • Skill or typing tests where prescribed

SSC Multi-Tasking Staff — MTS

SSC MTS recruitment covers notified Multi-Tasking Staff posts. Eligibility and selection requirements must be checked for each recruitment cycle.

Broad preparation areas

  • Numerical and Mathematical Ability
  • Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving
  • General Awareness
  • English Language and Comprehension

SSC GD Constable

SSC GD recruitment is conducted for notified constable positions. The selection process may include computer-based examination, physical tests and medical examination.

Broad preparation areas

  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Knowledge and General Awareness
  • Elementary Mathematics
  • English or Hindi as prescribed
  • Physical standards and physical efficiency preparation

SSC Central Police Organisation — CPO

SSC CPO recruitment is associated with notified Sub-Inspector positions. Candidates should check the examination and physical requirements in the applicable notification.

Broad preparation areas

  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Knowledge and General Awareness
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • English Comprehension
  • Physical tests and medical standards where applicable

SSC Selection Post

Selection Post recruitment covers different vacancies and qualification levels. The subject requirements vary according to the notified post.

Preparation approach

  • Review the qualification level
  • Identify the prescribed examination subjects
  • Study the relevant General Awareness topics
  • Practise subject-specific questions
  • Check any additional post requirements
Important These are broad preparation areas. Do not treat them as the exact current exam pattern. Paper structure, marks, negative marking, eligibility and selection stages must be verified using the relevant SSC notification.

YOUR STUDY JOURNEY

How to Prepare for SSC Exams

Step 1 — Choose the correct examination

Compare the notified posts, qualifications, age requirements and examination scheme.

Step 2 — Strengthen mathematics

Practise calculations and learn methods to solve numerical questions accurately.

Step 3 — Improve reasoning

Practise different reasoning question types and understand the underlying patterns.

Step 4 — Improve English

Study grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension and sentence structure.

Step 5 — Take timed practice tests

Analyse mistakes, improve speed and revise weak topics regularly.

DETAILED LEARNING GUIDE

SSC Subjects – Topic-wise Study Guide

Build concepts first, then practise questions under time limits.

1. Quantitative Aptitude / గణిత సామర్థ్యం

English: Begin with number systems, simplification, decimals and fractions. Continue with percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, profit and loss, discount, simple and compound interest, partnership, mixture and allegation, time and work, pipes and cisterns, time-speed-distance, trains, boats and streams, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, data interpretation and basic statistics. The exact depth differs by examination.

తెలుగు: సంఖ్యా వ్యవస్థ, సరళీకరణ, దశాంశాలు, భిన్నాలతో ప్రారంభించండి. తరువాత శాతాలు, నిష్పత్తి, సగటు, లాభనష్టాలు, తగ్గింపు, సాధారణ మరియు చక్రవడ్డీ, భాగస్వామ్యం, మిశ్రమాలు, కాలం-పని, పైపులు, వేగం-దూరం-కాలం, బీజగణితం, జ్యామితి, క్షేత్రమితి, త్రికోణమితి, డేటా విశ్లేషణను అభ్యసించండి.

Practice order / అభ్యాస క్రమం

  1. Tables, squares, cubes and fraction-to-percentage conversions.
  2. One arithmetic topic at a time; solve basic, moderate and timed questions.
  3. Maintain a formula notebook and revise it weekly.

2. General Intelligence & Reasoning / తార్కిక సామర్థ్యం

English: Analogy, classification, number and alphabet series, coding-decoding, direction and distance, blood relations, order and ranking, syllogism, Venn diagrams, mathematical operations, statement-conclusion, paper folding and cutting, embedded figures, mirror and water images, non-verbal reasoning and basic puzzles.

తెలుగు: సాదృశ్యాలు, వర్గీకరణ, సంఖ్యా-అక్షర శ్రేణులు, కోడింగ్-డీకోడింగ్, దిశలు, రక్త సంబంధాలు, క్రమం-ర్యాంకింగ్, సిలోజిజం, వెన్ డయాగ్రామ్స్, గణిత చిహ్నాల మార్పులు, ప్రకటన-నిర్ధారణ, కాగితం మడతలు, దాచిన ఆకారాలు, అద్దం-నీటి ప్రతిబింబాలు వంటి అంశాలను చదవండి.

Method: Identify the rule before looking at options. Record the reason for every wrong answer.

3. English Language / ఆంగ్ల భాష

English: Parts of speech, subject-verb agreement, tenses, articles, prepositions, conjunctions, pronouns, adjectives, adverbs, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech, sentence correction, error spotting, fill in the blanks, cloze test, synonyms, antonyms, idioms and phrases, one-word substitution, spelling and reading comprehension.

తెలుగు: Parts of Speech, Subject-Verb Agreement, కాలాలు, Articles, Prepositions, Conjunctions, Pronouns, Active-Passive Voice, Direct-Indirect Speech, వాక్య సవరణ, తప్పుల గుర్తింపు, ఖాళీల పూరణ, Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms, One-word Substitution, Spelling, Reading Comprehension అభ్యసించండి.

Read a short English passage daily. Learn words in context and revise them instead of memorising isolated lists.

4. General Awareness / సాధారణ అవగాహన

  • History: Ancient, Medieval and Modern Indian history; freedom movement and important personalities.
  • Geography: Physical geography, Indian rivers, climate, soils, agriculture, resources and maps.
  • Polity: Constitution, Preamble, Fundamental Rights and Duties, Directive Principles, Parliament, President, Prime Minister, judiciary and constitutional bodies.
  • Economy: Basic economic terms, national income, inflation, banking, budget, taxation and government programmes.
  • General Science: Everyday Physics, basic Chemistry, Biology, human body, nutrition, diseases and environment.
  • Current Affairs: National and international events, awards, sports, books, appointments, science and technology.

తెలుగు: భారత చరిత్ర, భౌగోళికం, రాజ్యాంగం, ఆర్థిక వ్యవస్థ, ప్రాథమిక భౌతిక-రసాయన-జీవ శాస్త్రాలు, పర్యావరణం మరియు సమకాలీన అంశాలను క్రమం తప్పకుండా చదవండి.

5. Computer Knowledge / కంప్యూటర్ పరిజ్ఞానం

Where a notification prescribes computer knowledge, study computer fundamentals, hardware and software, input/output devices, memory and storage, operating systems, file management, word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, internet, email, networking, cybersecurity, digital payments and common computer terminology.

తెలుగు: నోటిఫికేషన్‌లో కంప్యూటర్ పరిజ్ఞానం ఉంటే కంప్యూటర్ ప్రాథమికాలు, హార్డ్‌వేర్, సాఫ్ట్‌వేర్, మెమరీ, ఆపరేటింగ్ సిస్టమ్, MS Office/సమాన సాధనాలు, ఇంటర్నెట్, ఈ-మెయిల్, నెట్‌వర్కింగ్, సైబర్ భద్రత, డిజిటల్ చెల్లింపులు చదవండి.

CHOOSE YOUR LEVEL

Which SSC Examination Should You Explore?

Graduate-level route – CGL

For notified posts requiring a bachelor's degree. Read the post-wise educational qualifications, age limits and Tier-wise scheme in the current CGL notice. Some posts may have additional subject, physical or skill requirements.

Higher-secondary route – CHSL

For notified LDC/JSA and DEO-type posts where the required qualification is generally 10+2, subject to the post-specific conditions in the notice. Typing or data-entry skill requirements apply as specified.

Matriculation-level route – MTS / Havaldar

For notified posts with the prescribed matriculation qualification. Havaldar recruitment can include physical standards and physical efficiency tests. Check the exact notice for each post.

Constable and Sub-Inspector routes – GD / CPO

These recruitments have their own educational, age, physical and medical standards. Prepare for the computer-based paper and begin safe, gradual physical conditioning only after reviewing the official standards.

Eligibility reminderThese are broad route descriptions, not a personal eligibility decision. Age cut-off date, category relaxation, qualification date, nationality conditions, physical standards and post preferences are defined by each recruitment notice.
PRACTICAL ROUTINE

Weekly SSC Study Plan

  1. Monday: Quantitative Aptitude concepts + 25 practice questions.
  2. Tuesday: Reasoning concepts + 30 practice questions.
  3. Wednesday: English grammar + vocabulary + comprehension.
  4. Thursday: History and Polity + short revision notes.
  5. Friday: Geography, Economy and General Science.
  6. Saturday: Current Affairs revision + mixed sectional tests.
  7. Sunday: One timed mock test, error analysis and weekly revision.

Adjust the number of questions and study hours to your available time. Accuracy and consistent revision matter more than rushing through topics.

తెలుగు సూచన: ప్రతి రోజు ఒక ప్రధాన సబ్జెక్ట్‌పై దృష్టి పెట్టండి. తప్పుల నోటుబుక్ నిర్వహించండి. వారానికి ఒక మాక్ టెస్ట్ రాసి, తప్పుల కారణాలను గుర్తించి మళ్లీ చదవండి.
