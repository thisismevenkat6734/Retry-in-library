CONTENT QUALITY & ACCOUNTABILITY

Corrections & Content Removal

Help us identify errors, outdated information, broken links and content concerns so we can improve the learning experience.

Important: Reporting is not active yet

A verified public contact channel and submission system have not yet been configured. This page explains the intended reporting process but does not currently submit or send reports.

1. What Can You Report?

We welcome specific reports about problems in our educational content and website.

  • Incorrect facts, dates, names or explanations.
  • Incorrect MCQ answers or explanations.
  • Outdated current-affairs information.
  • Incorrect or unverified previous-year question labels.
  • Broken internal or external links.
  • Missing source references or attribution.
  • Accessibility or display problems.
  • Copyright or other intellectual property concerns.
  • Privacy or security concerns related to the website.

2. Information to Include in a Report

To help us investigate a concern, a report should include as much relevant information as possible.

  1. Page URL: Provide the exact website address where the issue appears.
  2. Content location: Mention the chapter, heading, question number or paragraph.
  3. Issue description: Explain what appears to be incorrect or problematic.
  4. Suggested correction: If possible, explain what should be changed.
  5. Supporting source: Include an official notification, reliable reference or original source link.
  6. Date: Mention when you noticed the issue, especially for time-sensitive information.

3. Reporting an Incorrect MCQ

For a question or answer issue, please provide:

  • Exam and subject category, if applicable.
  • Question text or question number.
  • Answer currently shown on the website.
  • Answer you believe is correct.
  • A reliable explanation or reference supporting the correction.

Questions and explanations will need editorial review before any correction is published.

4. Reporting Outdated Current Affairs

Current-affairs information can change after publication. Please include:

  • The exact entry or topic.
  • The date of the original information.
  • The latest official update, if available.
  • A link to the official announcement or report.

Historical information should not be silently changed in a way that makes its original publication date unclear.

5. Copyright and Content Removal Requests

If you believe that content on this website infringes your copyright or other rights, please refer to our Copyright Policy.

A detailed report should identify the material, the relevant page URL, your basis for making the request and supporting evidence.

We will review reports and take appropriate action where warranted. A report does not automatically establish infringement or guarantee removal.

6. Privacy and Sensitive Information

Do not include passwords, payment details, government identity numbers, private student records or other unnecessary sensitive personal information in a report.

If a screenshot is necessary, remove unrelated personal information before sharing it.

7. How Reports Will Be Reviewed

Once a working reporting channel is established, reports are intended to follow this review process:

  1. Receive and identify the reported page or material.
  2. Check the issue and supporting references.
  3. Compare the information with reliable original sources.
  4. Correct, clarify, update or remove material where appropriate.
  5. Record the change and relevant source information.

Review time may vary depending on the complexity of the issue. No response or resolution time is guaranteed.

8. No False or Abusive Reports

Reports should be submitted honestly and in good faith. Do not knowingly provide false information, impersonate another person or use the reporting process to harass others.

9. Reporting Channel Status

A verified public email address, contact form or ticket system has not yet been published.

The Contact page currently provides project information but does not offer a functioning report-submission service.

Please do not assume that a report has been received unless a working submission channel confirms it.
