Content Sources & Attribution

Learn where our educational references come from and how we identify, verify and attribute information.

Important transparency notice

The websites listed below are potential reference sources for relevant subjects. Their inclusion does not mean that every page or fact on RETRY IN LIBRARY has already been checked against them. Individual lessons should identify the sources actually used.

1. Our Source Selection Principles

We aim to use reliable, relevant and traceable references while preparing educational material.

  • Prefer official government and examination-authority publications for rules, notifications and public data.
  • Use recognised educational resources for foundational concepts.
  • Check publication dates for current affairs and changing information.
  • Distinguish original practice questions from verified previous-year questions.
  • Record source details close to the relevant lesson wherever practical.
  • Respect copyright, licences and attribution requirements.

6. How Sources Should Be Recorded

Each published chapter or current-affairs entry should maintain a source record. The following fields are recommended for our editorial workflow.

Field What to record
Content title Name of the chapter, question set or current-affairs entry.
Publisher Government department, institution, author or organisation.
Source title Exact report, notification, textbook or webpage title.
Source URL Direct link to the referenced material, where available.
Publication date Date shown by the original publisher, if available.
Accessed date Date the editorial team checked the source.
Licence / reuse terms Applicable licence, permission or reuse conditions.
Verification status Reviewed, needs review or outdated.

7. Original Content and Attribution

Our goal is to explain concepts in independently written language rather than reproduce substantial portions of copyrighted publications.

When third-party material is used under a licence or permission, the applicable attribution and reuse conditions must be followed.

A source link alone does not grant permission to copy protected content.

8. Previous-Year Questions and Question Sources

Questions should be identified accurately according to their origin.

  • Original questions must be labelled as original practice questions.
  • Verified previous-year questions should include the examination, year and available official source reference.
  • Questions with uncertain origins must not be presented as verified official questions.
  • Any applicable copyright or reuse restrictions must be checked before publication.

9. Corrections and Source Suggestions

If you find an incorrect reference, broken link, outdated source or missing attribution, please visit our Corrections & Content Removal page.
