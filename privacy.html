Privacy Policy

How RETRY IN LIBRARY handles browser-stored information and explains privacy-related practices.

Website: RETRY IN LIBRARY
Effective date: 13 November 2026
Last updated: 13 November 2026

This is a preliminary privacy policy draft. It must be checked against the website's actual hosting, advertising, analytics and third-party service configuration before publication. Obtain appropriate legal review, including review under applicable Indian data-protection law.

1. Introduction

RETRY IN LIBRARY is an independent educational website intended to support students and competitive examination aspirants.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be handled when you access or use the website, including information stored locally in your browser.

The actual data practices may change if new features, advertising services, analytics tools or other integrations are introduced.

2. No User Account Required for Current Learning Features

The currently implemented learning pages are designed to operate without requiring users to create a website account.

The current design does not require a user profile, password or cloud-based learning account for the basic MCQ and mock-test features.

If account-based features are introduced in the future, this policy must be updated before those features are made available.

3. Information Stored in Your Browser

Some features may use browser LocalStorage to save information such as:

  • Mock-test results and test history.
  • MCQ practice statistics, where implemented.
  • Display preferences such as theme or language settings.

This information is stored in the browser on the device you use. It is not automatically uploaded to a RETRY IN LIBRARY cloud database.

Browser storage may be removed when you clear site data, change browsers, use private browsing or reset your device.

4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

The currently implemented basic learning features do not require you to provide:

  • Your full name.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your home address.
  • Your government identity number.
  • Your examination login credentials.
  • Your bank account or payment information.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into any website field unless a clearly explained feature requires it.

5. Hosting and Technical Information

The website is intended to be hosted as a static website. The hosting provider and its infrastructure may process technical information necessary to deliver pages and maintain security.

Depending on the hosting configuration, technical records may include IP addresses, browser information, request timestamps and requested resources.

RETRY IN LIBRARY does not currently claim that it independently collects or stores all such technical records. The hosting provider's actual practices and retention rules must be checked in its own privacy documentation.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website's current learning features primarily use browser LocalStorage for certain preferences and practice data.

LocalStorage is different from browser cookies. It is not automatically sent to the website server with every request.

Hosting infrastructure, third-party services or advertising integrations may use cookies or similar technologies if enabled. Their actual use must be disclosed when implemented.

7. Analytics

This policy does not claim that a website analytics service is currently installed or active.

If analytics tools such as Google Analytics are introduced, this page should be updated to explain the information collected, purposes, third-party processing, retention and available controls.

Users should also be given any notices or choices required by applicable law.

8. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The website may introduce third-party advertising services or other external integrations in the future.

If advertising is enabled, the relevant provider may process information such as cookies, device identifiers, browser information or advertising interactions, subject to its own policies and applicable law.

This policy must be updated with the actual provider names, relevant data practices, consent mechanisms and user controls before advertising integrations are activated.

9. Third-Party Links

RETRY IN LIBRARY may link to government websites, examination authorities, educational resources and other external websites.

Once you visit an external website, its privacy policy and data practices apply. We do not control how those external services collect or use information.

Review third-party privacy policies before sharing information with those services.

10. Offline Access and Service Workers

The website may use a service worker to cache selected website resources for improved loading and limited offline access.

Cached files may remain in your browser until they are updated, removed or cleared by the browser.

Offline access is limited to resources that have already been cached or downloaded. It does not mean that every website page is permanently available offline.

11. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate technical measures for the website architecture and its implemented features.

However, no website, browser, hosting service or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Keep your device and browser updated, protect access to your device and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Browser-stored learning data may remain on your device until you clear the relevant browser storage or remove the data using an available website control.

You can use the browser's site-data settings to clear LocalStorage for this website. Doing so may also remove saved preferences and learning history.

Technical information held by hosting or third-party providers is subject to their own retention practices.

13. Children's Privacy

The website provides general educational resources. We do not currently require users to create accounts for the basic learning features.

If features involving personal information from children are introduced, they must be designed and operated in accordance with applicable child-protection and data-protection requirements.

We do not intend to collect children's personal information unnecessarily.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the actual data processing involved, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information.

These may include, where applicable:

  • Requesting information about personal data processing.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally available.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raising a grievance through an appropriate channel.

The exact rights, exceptions, processes and response obligations must be assessed under applicable law.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when website features, technology, third-party integrations or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

16. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy-related questions, requests or concerns, refer to the Contact Us page.

A verified public privacy contact channel has not yet been published. It must be added before the website is treated as fully operational.
