About Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh is a state in southeastern India, situated along the Bay of Bengal. Its history, agriculture, river systems, coastline, cultural heritage and administrative developments are important areas of study for state-level competitive examinations.

ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్ భారతదేశంలోని ఆగ్నేయ ప్రాంతంలో, బంగాళాఖాతం తీరాన ఉన్న రాష్ట్రం. రాష్ట్ర చరిత్ర, వ్యవసాయం, నదులు, తీరప్రాంతం, సాంస్కృతిక వారసత్వం మరియు పరిపాలనా అంశాలు పోటీ పరీక్షలకు ముఖ్యమైనవి.

1. History of Andhra Pradesh – ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్ చరిత్ర

Ancient History – ప్రాచీన చరిత్ర

The Andhra region has a long recorded history. Several dynasties and political powers contributed to its political, economic, religious and cultural development.

ఆంధ్ర ప్రాంతానికి సుదీర్ఘ చరిత్ర ఉంది. అనేక రాజవంశాలు ఈ ప్రాంత రాజకీయ, ఆర్థిక, మత మరియు సాంస్కృతిక అభివృద్ధికి తోడ్పడ్డాయి.

Important Dynasties – ముఖ్యమైన రాజవంశాలు

  • Satavahanas: An important ancient Deccan dynasty associated with the Andhra region.
  • Ikshvakus: Ruled parts of the Krishna valley region.
  • Eastern Chalukyas: Played a significant role in the history of Vengi.
  • Kakatiyas: An important medieval dynasty in the Telugu-speaking region.
  • Vijayanagara Empire: Influenced the political and cultural history of southern India.

Medieval and Modern Period

  • Regional kingdoms and changing political powers.
  • Vijayanagara and its legacy.
  • European trading companies and colonial expansion.
  • British administration in parts of the Andhra region.
  • Social reform and educational movements.

Freedom Movement – స్వాతంత్ర్య ఉద్యమం

The Andhra region participated in the Indian freedom struggle through political mobilisation, public movements, social reform and participation in national campaigns.

భారత స్వాతంత్ర్య ఉద్యమంలో ఆంధ్ర ప్రాంత ప్రజలు రాజకీయ చైతన్యం, సామాజిక సంస్కరణలు మరియు జాతీయ ఉద్యమాల ద్వారా పాల్గొన్నారు.

Preparation focus: Important personalities, movements, organisations, events, historical places and their chronology.

2. State Formation and Reorganisation

Formation of Andhra State

Andhra State was formed on 1 October 1953, following the movement for a separate state for Telugu-speaking people. Kurnool served as its capital.

తెలుగు మాట్లాడే ప్రజల ప్రత్యేక రాష్ట్ర ఉద్యమం ఫలితంగా 1953 అక్టోబర్ 1న ఆంధ్ర రాష్ట్రం ఏర్పడింది. కర్నూలు అప్పటి రాజధానిగా పనిచేసింది.

Formation of Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh was formed on 1 November 1956, following the merger of Andhra State with the Telugu-speaking areas of the erstwhile Hyderabad State under the States Reorganisation process.

రాష్ట్రాల పునర్వ్యవస్థీకరణ ప్రక్రియలో భాగంగా అప్పటి హైదరాబాద్ రాష్ట్రంలోని తెలుగు మాట్లాడే ప్రాంతాలు ఆంధ్ర రాష్ట్రంతో విలీనం కావడంతో 1956 నవంబర్ 1న ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్ ఏర్పడింది.

Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014

The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 provided the legal framework for the creation of Telangana and the reorganisation of the erstwhile state.

ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్ పునర్వ్యవస్థీకరణ చట్టం, 2014 తెలంగాణ రాష్ట్ర ఏర్పాటుకు మరియు ఉమ్మడి రాష్ట్ర పునర్వ్యవస్థీకరణకు చట్టపరమైన ఆధారాన్ని కల్పించింది.

Important Dates for Revision

  • 1 October 1953 – Formation of Andhra State.
  • 1 November 1956 – Formation of Andhra Pradesh.
  • 2 June 2014 – Telangana came into existence.

Exam preparation note: Study the official Act, parliamentary provisions, schedules and subsequent amendments separately. Do not rely only on short summaries for legal or constitutional questions.

3. Geography of Andhra Pradesh – భౌగోళిక స్వరూపం

Location and Boundaries

Andhra Pradesh is located on the southeastern side of India. The Bay of Bengal lies along its eastern coast.

Major Geographical Features

  • Eastern Coastal Plains – తూర్పు తీర మైదానాలు
  • Eastern Ghats – తూర్పు కనుమలు
  • River valleys – నదీ లోయలు
  • Plateau and upland regions – పీఠభూమి మరియు ఎత్తైన ప్రాంతాలు
  • Coastal and delta regions – తీర మరియు డెల్టా ప్రాంతాలు

Climate

The state experiences tropical climatic conditions. Rainfall patterns are influenced by the southwest and northeast monsoons, regional geography and cyclonic systems over the Bay of Bengal.

Important Geography Topics

  • Latitude and longitude of the state.
  • Coastal geography and major coastal settlements.
  • Eastern Ghats and important hill ranges.
  • Soil types and their agricultural suitability.
  • Rainfall distribution and climatic regions.
  • Natural vegetation and biodiversity.
  • Natural disasters and disaster management.

4. Rivers and Irrigation – నదులు మరియు నీటిపారుదల

Major River Systems

  • Godavari: One of the major rivers of peninsular India, with an extensive delta system.
  • Krishna: An important river for irrigation, agriculture and water-resource projects.
  • Penna: An important river system in the southern part of the state.
  • Vamsadhara: An important river in the northern coastal region.
  • Nagavali: A river system associated with the northern coastal districts.
  • Swarnamukhi: A river associated with the southern part of the state.

Important Irrigation Topics

  • Major and medium irrigation projects.
  • Canal systems and command areas.
  • Reservoirs and dams.
  • Inter-state river-water disputes.
  • Groundwater resources.
  • Water conservation and watershed development.

Projects to Study

  • Polavaram Irrigation Project.
  • Prakasam Barrage.
  • Srisailam Project.
  • Nagarjuna Sagar Project.
  • Telugu Ganga Project.
  • Handri-Neeva Sujala Sravanthi.
  • Galeru-Nagari Sujala Sravanthi.

Project status, capacity, cost, completion stages and water allocations can change. Verify such facts from the latest government reports before using them in an examination.

5. Districts and Administration – జిల్లాలు మరియు పరిపాలన

Administrative Structure

State administration operates through constitutional, statutory and local-government institutions.

  • Governor – గవర్నర్
  • Chief Minister and Council of Ministers – ముఖ్యమంత్రి మరియు మంత్రిమండలి
  • State Legislature – రాష్ట్ర శాసనసభ
  • High Court and subordinate judiciary – న్యాయవ్యవస్థ
  • District administration – జిల్లా పరిపాలన
  • Municipal corporations and municipalities – పట్టణ స్థానిక సంస్థలు
  • Gram Panchayats and rural local bodies – గ్రామీణ స్థానిక సంస్థలు

District Administration

District-level administration coordinates revenue functions, development programmes, public services and implementation of government policies.

Important administrative roles include:

  • District Collector
  • Superintendent of Police
  • District-level departmental officers
  • Revenue Divisional Officer
  • Mandal-level administrative officers

Local Self-Government

  • 73rd Constitutional Amendment – Panchayati Raj institutions.
  • 74th Constitutional Amendment – Urban local bodies.
  • Gram Panchayat – Village-level local government.
  • Mandal Parishad – Intermediate rural local-government structure.
  • Zilla Parishad – District-level rural local-government structure.

District names, district count, headquarters, revenue divisions and mandal boundaries are changeable administrative facts. Check the latest Andhra Pradesh government notification before memorising these details.

6. Economy and Agriculture – ఆర్థిక వ్యవస్థ మరియు వ్యవసాయం

Major Economic Sectors

  • Agriculture and allied activities.
  • Industry and manufacturing.
  • Services and trade.
  • Transport, logistics and tourism.
  • Fisheries and aquaculture.

Agriculture

Agriculture is an important part of Andhra Pradesh's economy. Irrigation availability, soil, rainfall, market access and agricultural technology influence crop production.

Important Crops

  • Rice – వరి
  • Maize – మొక్కజొన్న
  • Groundnut – వేరుశెనగ
  • Cotton – పత్తి
  • Chilli – మిరప
  • Tobacco – పొగాకు
  • Sugarcane – చెరకు
  • Pulses – పప్పుధాన్యాలు
  • Horticultural crops – ఉద్యాన పంటలు

Allied Activities

  • Dairy and livestock.
  • Inland and marine fisheries.
  • Aquaculture and shrimp farming.
  • Poultry and animal husbandry.

Economic Indicators to Study

  • Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP).
  • Per capita income.
  • Sector-wise contribution to the economy.
  • Employment and unemployment.
  • Agricultural productivity.
  • State budget and public expenditure.
  • Exports and industrial investment.

Economic statistics must always be studied with the financial year, publication date, data source and whether the figure is an advance estimate, provisional estimate or revised estimate.

8. Culture and Heritage – సంస్కృతి మరియు వారసత్వం

Languages and Literature

Telugu is the principal language of Andhra Pradesh. Telugu literature has a rich tradition spanning poetry, prose, classical works and modern writing.

  • Veeranatyam – వీరనాట్యం

Festivals and Traditions
