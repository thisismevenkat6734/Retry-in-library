About Telangana – తెలంగాణ పరిచయం

Telangana is a state in southern India. It became the 29th state of India on 2 June 2014, following the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014. Hyderabad is its capital.

తెలంగాణ భారతదేశంలోని దక్షిణ ప్రాంతంలో ఉన్న రాష్ట్రం. ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్ పునర్వ్యవస్థీకరణ చట్టం, 2014 ప్రకారం 2014 జూన్ 2న భారతదేశ 29వ రాష్ట్రంగా ఏర్పడింది. హైదరాబాద్ దీని రాజధాని.

1. History of Telangana – తెలంగాణ చరిత్ర

Ancient History – ప్రాచీన చరిత్ర

The Telangana region has a long historical background. Various dynasties and kingdoms influenced its political, religious, economic and cultural development.

Important Dynasties – ముఖ్యమైన రాజవంశాలు

  • Satavahanas: An important ancient Deccan dynasty.
  • Vakatakas: An important dynasty in the Deccan and central Indian historical context.
  • Vishnukundins: Ruled parts of the eastern Deccan.
  • Chalukyas: Influenced the political and cultural history of the Deccan.
  • Kakatiyas: An important dynasty associated with Warangal and the Telugu-speaking region.
  • Qutb Shahis: Ruled the Golconda kingdom and developed Hyderabad.
  • Asaf Jahis: The Nizams ruled the Hyderabad State.

Kakatiya Dynasty – కాకతీయులు

The Kakatiyas played a major role in the history of the Telangana region. Warangal was an important political centre during their rule.

Important topics to study:

  • Rudrama Devi
  • Prataparudra
  • Ganapatideva
  • Warangal Fort
  • Ramappa Temple
  • Irrigation tanks and water management

Qutb Shahi Period

  • Golconda Fort and its historical importance.
  • Development of Hyderabad city.
  • Charminar and related historical events.
  • Architecture, trade and cultural developments.

Asaf Jahi and Hyderabad State

The Asaf Jahi dynasty governed the Hyderabad State for a long period. The political and administrative history of the Nizams is important for understanding modern Telangana.

Important Modern History Topics

  • Hyderabad State under the Nizams.
  • Social and educational reform movements.
  • Peasant movements and the Telangana armed struggle.
  • Integration of Hyderabad State into the Indian Union in 1948.
  • Formation of Andhra Pradesh in 1956.

2. Telangana Statehood Movement – తెలంగాణ ఉద్యమం

Background

The Telangana statehood movement developed around political, economic, administrative and cultural concerns of the region. Issues related to employment, public resources, education and regional development became important themes.

తెలంగాణ ప్రత్యేక రాష్ట్ర ఉద్యమం ప్రాంతీయ అభివృద్ధి, ఉద్యోగాలు, విద్య, నీటి వనరులు, పరిపాలన మరియు సాంస్కృతిక గుర్తింపు వంటి అంశాల చుట్టూ అభివృద్ధి చెందింది.

Important Phases

  • Regional concerns before and after the formation of Andhra Pradesh.
  • 1969 Telangana movement.
  • Political and public mobilisation in later decades.
  • Formation of Telangana Rashtra Samithi in 2001.
  • Renewed mass mobilisation during 2009–2014.
  • Parliamentary process leading to state formation.

Important Study Areas

  • Gentlemen's Agreement and its historical context.
  • Mulki rules and employment-related issues.
  • Six-Point Formula.
  • Regional committees and political developments.
  • Public movements and student participation.
  • Parliamentary debates and legislation.

For examination answers, distinguish between historical events, political demands, legal provisions and later interpretations. Verify dates and provisions from original documents.

3. Formation of Telangana – తెలంగాణ రాష్ట్ర ఆవిర్భావం

Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014

The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 provided the legal framework for the formation of Telangana from the erstwhile state of Andhra Pradesh.

Important Date

2 June 2014 – Telangana became a separate state.

Important Topics

  • Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014.
  • Creation of Telangana as a separate state.
  • Capital and administrative arrangements.
  • Division of institutions and public assets.
  • River-water and resource-related provisions.
  • Representation and constitutional arrangements.

Study the original Act, schedules and subsequent legal developments for questions involving specific provisions.

4. Geography and Climate – భౌగోళిక స్వరూపం

Location

Telangana is a landlocked state in southern India. It is situated mainly on the Deccan Plateau.

Major Geographical Features

  • Deccan Plateau – దక్కన్ పీఠభూమి
  • River valleys – నదీ లోయలు
  • Granite rock formations – గ్రానైట్ శిలా నిర్మాణాలు
  • Forest regions – అటవీ ప్రాంతాలు
  • Plateau uplands – పీఠభూమి ఎత్తైన ప్రాంతాలు

Climate

Telangana generally experiences a tropical climate with hot summers, monsoon rainfall and relatively cooler winters. Rainfall is influenced mainly by the southwest monsoon.

Geography Topics

  • Location and neighbouring states.
  • Major physiographic regions.
  • Soil types and agricultural regions.
  • Rainfall distribution.
  • Forests and biodiversity.
  • Natural resources and minerals.
  • Environmental issues and disaster management.

5. Rivers and Irrigation Projects – నదులు మరియు ప్రాజెక్టులు

Major Rivers

  • Godavari: A major river flowing through northern Telangana and supporting irrigation and water supply.
  • Krishna: An important river along the southern part of the state.
  • Manjira: An important tributary of the Godavari.
  • Pranahita: A major tributary of the Godavari system.
  • MusI: A river associated with Hyderabad.
  • Bhima: Part of the Krishna river system.
  • Penganga: Part of the Godavari river system.

Important Projects to Study

  • Kaleshwaram Lift Irrigation Project.
  • Sri Ram Sagar Project.
  • Nagarjuna Sagar Project.
  • Srisailam Project.
  • Nizam Sagar Project.
  • Singur Project.
  • Jurala Project.
  • Lower Manair Dam.
  • Mid Manair Dam.

Important Concepts

  • River basin and catchment area.
  • Major and minor irrigation.
  • Lift irrigation technology.
  • Inter-state river-water disputes.
  • Groundwater management.
  • Tank irrigation and traditional water systems.

Project capacity, current operating status, construction stages, costs and water allocations require verification from current official irrigation department publications.
