1. Introduction to Percentage | శాతాల పరిచయం

English: Percentage is a way of expressing a number or quantity as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred". It is represented by the symbol %.

తెలుగు: ప్రతి వందకు ఎంత అనే భావనను వ్యక్తీకరించే విధానాన్ని శాతం అంటారు. శాతం అనే పదానికి వందకు అని అర్థం. దీనిని % గుర్తుతో సూచిస్తారు.

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100
Example: A student scores 40 marks out of 50.

Percentage = (40/50) × 100
= 0.8 × 100
= 80%

Answer: 80%
తెలుగు: 50 మార్కులకు 40 మార్కులు వస్తే శాతం 80%.

Importance in Competitive Exams | పోటీ పరీక్షల్లో ప్రాముఖ్యత

  • Marks and examination results – పరీక్ష మార్కులు.
  • Population growth – జనాభా పెరుగుదల.
  • Profit and loss – లాభనష్టాలు.
  • Discount calculations – తగ్గింపులు.
  • Simple and compound interest – సాధారణ, చక్రవడ్డీ.
  • Data interpretation – గణాంకాల విశ్లేషణ.
  • Salary and price changes – జీతాలు, ధరల మార్పులు.
Percentage is a foundation topic for Profit and Loss, Discount, Interest and Data Interpretation.
శాతాలు అనేవి లాభనష్టాలు, తగ్గింపులు, వడ్డీ మరియు గణాంకాల విశ్లేషణకు పునాది.

2. Basic Concepts of Percentage | శాతాల ప్రాథమిక భావనలు

2.1 Meaning of Percentage

Percentage means a value out of 100.

వందలో ఒక భాగాన్ని శాతంగా వ్యక్తీకరిస్తారు.

PercentageMeaning
1%1 out of 100
10%10 out of 100
25%25 out of 100
50%50 out of 100
75%75 out of 100
100%Whole quantity
150%One and a half times the quantity
200%Twice the quantity

2.2 Basic Formula

Percentage = (Obtained Value / Total Value) × 100
Question: A student gets 72 marks out of 90. Find the percentage.

Percentage = (72/90) × 100
= 0.8 × 100
= 80%

Answer: 80%

2.3 Finding a Percentage of a Number

x% of y = (x/100) × y
Question: Find 20% of 500.

= (20/100) × 500
= 100

Answer: 100

2.4 Finding the Whole Quantity

Whole = (Part × 100) / Percentage
Question: 40% of a number is 120. Find the number.

Number = (120 × 100)/40
= 12000/40
= 300

Answer: 300

2.5 Finding the Percentage of Remaining Quantity

A person spends 30% of their income. What percentage remains?

Remaining = 100% − 30%
= 70%

Answer: 70%

3. Conversion of Fractions, Decimals and Percentages | మార్పిడులు

3.1 Fraction to Percentage | భిన్నం నుండి శాతం

Percentage = Fraction × 100
Convert 3/4 into percentage.

= (3/4) × 100
= 75%

Answer: 75%

3.2 Percentage to Fraction | శాతం నుండి భిన్నం

Write the percentage over 100 and simplify.

శాతాన్ని 100తో భాగించి, భిన్నాన్ని సరళీకరించాలి.

Convert 40% into a fraction.

= 40/100
= 2/5

Answer: 2/5

3.3 Decimal to Percentage | దశాంశం నుండి శాతం

Decimal × 100 = Percentage
Convert 0.75 into percentage.

= 0.75 × 100
= 75%

Answer: 75%

3.4 Percentage to Decimal | శాతం నుండి దశాంశం

Percentage ÷ 100 = Decimal
Convert 65% into decimal.

= 65/100
= 0.65

Answer: 0.65

4. Percentage Calculations | శాతం గణనలు

4.1 Finding x% of a Number

x% of y = (x × y)/100
Find 15% of 800.

= (15 × 800)/100
= 15 × 8
= 120

Answer: 120

4.2 Finding What Percentage One Number is of Another

Required Percentage = (First Number / Second Number) × 100
40 is what percentage of 200?

= (40/200) × 100
= 20%

Answer: 20%

4.3 Finding the Original Number

25% of a number is 75. Find the number.

Number = (75 × 100)/25
= 300

Answer: 300

4.4 Comparing Two Numbers

A is 50 and B is 100. A is what percentage of B?

= (50/100) × 100
= 50%

Answer: 50%

4.5 Percentage of a Total

A class has 40 students. 24 are boys. Find the percentage of boys.

= (24/40) × 100
= 60%

Answer: 60%

5. Percentage Increase and Decrease | శాతం పెరుగుదల మరియు తగ్గుదల

5.1 Percentage Increase | శాతం పెరుగుదల

Percentage Increase = (Increase / Original Value) × 100
A price increases from ₹200 to ₹250. Find the percentage increase.

Increase = 250 − 200 = 50
Percentage Increase = (50/200) × 100
= 25%

Answer: 25%

5.2 Percentage Decrease | శాతం తగ్గుదల

Percentage Decrease = (Decrease / Original Value) × 100
A price decreases from ₹500 to ₹400.

Decrease = 500 − 400 = 100
Percentage Decrease = (100/500) × 100
= 20%

Answer: 20%

5.3 New Value after Increase

New Value = Original Value × (1 + r/100)
A salary of ₹10,000 increases by 10%.

New Salary = 10000 × (1 + 10/100)
= 10000 × 1.10
= ₹11,000

5.4 New Value after Decrease

New Value = Original Value × (1 − r/100)
A product costs ₹800. Its price decreases by 15%.

New Price = 800 × (1 − 15/100)
= 800 × 0.85
= ₹680

5.5 Important Difference

Percentage increase and percentage decrease are calculated using the original value as the denominator.

శాతం పెరుగుదల, తగ్గుదలలను లెక్కించేటప్పుడు అసలు విలువను హారంగా తీసుకోవాలి.

6. Successive Percentage Changes | వరుస శాతం మార్పులు

English: When a value changes by two or more percentages successively, each percentage is applied to the value obtained after the previous change.

తెలుగు: ఒక విలువపై వరుసగా రెండు లేదా అంతకంటే ఎక్కువ శాతం మార్పులు జరిగినప్పుడు, ప్రతి మార్పును ముందు వచ్చిన కొత్త విలువపై లెక్కించాలి.

6.1 Two Successive Increases

Net Increase = a + b + (ab/100) %
A value increases by 10% and then by 20%.

Net Increase = 10 + 20 + (10 × 20)/100
= 30 + 2
= 32%

Answer: 32% increase

6.2 Two Successive Decreases

Net Decrease = a + b − (ab/100) %
A value decreases by 10% and then by 20%.

Net Decrease = 10 + 20 − (10 × 20)/100
= 30 − 2
= 28%

Answer: 28% decrease

6.3 One Increase and One Decrease

Net Change = a − b − (ab/100) %

Here a is the increase percentage and b is the decrease percentage. The result may be positive or negative.

A value increases by 20% and then decreases by 10%.

Net Change = 20 − 10 − (20 × 10)/100
= 10 − 2
= 8%

Answer: 8% increase

6.4 General Successive Change Formula

Final Value = Original Value × (1 ± a/100) × (1 ± b/100)

Use + for increase and − for decrease.

A value of 1000 increases by 10% and then decreases by 10%.

= 1000 × 1.10 × 0.90
= 990

Net change = 990 − 1000 = −10
Percentage change = −1%

Answer: 1% decrease
Equal percentage increase and decrease do not cancel each other. A 10% increase followed by a 10% decrease results in a 1% net decrease.

ఒకే శాతం పెరుగుదల, తగ్గుదల పరస్పరం రద్దు కావు.

7. Applications of Percentage | శాతాల ఉపయోగాలు

7.1 Examination Marks | పరీక్ష మార్కులు

A student scores 450 out of 600.

Percentage = (450/600) × 100
= 75%

Answer: 75%

7.2 Population Growth | జనాభా పెరుగుదల

A town has a population of 20,000. It increases by 5%.

Increase = 5% of 20,000
= 1,000
New Population = 20,000 + 1,000
= 21,000

Answer: 21,000

7.3 Discount | తగ్గింపు

Discount is the reduction in the marked price of a product.

ఒక వస్తువు గుర్తించిన ధరపై ఇచ్చే తగ్గింపును డిస్కౌంట్ అంటారు.

Discount = Marked Price − Selling Price

Discount % = (Discount / Marked Price) × 100
Marked Price = ₹1,000
Discount = 20%

Discount Amount = 1000 × 20/100
= ₹200
Selling Price = 1000 − 200
= ₹800

7.4 Profit and Loss | లాభనష్టాలు

Percentage is used to calculate profit and loss relative to cost price.

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

Profit % = (Profit / Cost Price) × 100

Loss = Cost Price − Selling Price

Loss % = (Loss / Cost Price) × 100
Cost Price = ₹500
Selling Price = ₹600

Profit = 600 − 500 = ₹100
Profit % = (100/500) × 100
= 20%

7.5 Simple Interest | సాధారణ వడ్డీ

SI = (P × R × T)/100

P = Principal (అసలు)

R = Annual Interest Rate (వార్షిక వడ్డీ రేటు)

T = Time in Years (సంవత్సరాల్లో కాలం)

Principal = ₹5,000
Rate = 10% per annum
Time = 2 years

SI = (5000 × 10 × 2)/100
= ₹1,000

7.6 Salary Increase | జీతం పెరుగుదల

An employee earns ₹20,000. Salary increases by 15%.

Increase = 20,000 × 15/100
= ₹3,000
New Salary = ₹23,000

7.7 Election Results | ఎన్నికల ఫలితాలు

A candidate receives 6,000 votes out of 10,000 valid votes.

Percentage = (6000/10000) × 100
= 60%

Answer: 60%

8. Quick Calculation Shortcuts | వేగవంతమైన గణన పద్ధతులు

8.1 Finding 10%

Divide the number by 10.

సంఖ్యను 10తో భాగించాలి.

10% of 800 = 80

8.2 Finding 5%

Find 10% and divide by 2.

5% of 600
10% = 60
5% = 60/2 = 30

8.3 Finding 1%

Divide the number by 100.

1% of 900 = 9

8.4 Finding 25%

Divide the number by 4.

25% of 800 = 800/4 = 200

8.5 Finding 50%

Divide the number by 2.

50% of 760 = 380

8.6 Finding 75%

Find 3/4 of the number.

75% of 400
= 400 × 3/4
= 300

8.7 Finding 20%

Divide the number by 5.

20% of 350 = 70

8.8 Finding 12.5%

Divide the number by 8.

12.5% of 800 = 100

8.9 Finding 2.5%

Divide the number by 40.

2.5% of 800 = 20
