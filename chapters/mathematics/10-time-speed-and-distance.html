Mathematics – Time, Speed and Distance

సమయం, వేగం మరియు దూరం

Bilingual Study Material • Chapter 10

1. Introduction | పరిచయం

English: Time, Speed and Distance explains the relationship between how far an object travels, how fast it moves and how much time it takes.

తెలుగు: ఒక వస్తువు ఎంత దూరం ప్రయాణించింది, ఎంత వేగంతో ప్రయాణించింది, ఎంత సమయం పట్టింది అనే అంశాల మధ్య సంబంధాన్ని ఈ పాఠంలో తెలుసుకుంటాం.

These concepts are frequently used in competitive-exam questions involving vehicles, trains, boats, runners and average speed.

2. Important Formulas | ముఖ్యమైన సూత్రాలు

Speed = Distance ÷ Time
వేగం = దూరం ÷ సమయం
Distance = Speed × Time
దూరం = వేగం × సమయం
Time = Distance ÷ Speed
సమయం = దూరం ÷ వేగం
km/h to m/s: multiply by 5/18
m/s to km/h: multiply by 18/5
Relative speed:
Opposite directions: add the speeds.
Same direction: subtract the smaller speed from the greater speed.
Average speed = Total distance ÷ Total time
Equal distances at speeds a and b: average speed = 2ab/(a+b).
Train crossing a pole: time = train length ÷ speed.
Train crossing a platform: time = (train length + platform length) ÷ speed.
Boats: Downstream speed = still-water speed + stream speed.
Upstream speed = still-water speed − stream speed.
Stream speed = (downstream speed − upstream speed) ÷ 2.

4. Solved Examples | సాధించిన ఉదాహరణలు

Example 1

A bus covers 180 km in 3 hours. Find its speed.

Solution: Speed = 180 ÷ 3 = 60 km/h.

తెలుగు: వేగం = 180 ÷ 3 = గంటకు 60 కి.మీ.

Example 2

Convert 90 km/h into m/s.

Solution: 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s.

Example 3

Two vehicles move in opposite directions at 35 km/h and 45 km/h. Find relative speed.

Solution: 35 + 45 = 80 km/h.

Example 4

A 120 m train crosses a pole in 8 seconds.

Solution: Speed = 120 ÷ 8 = 15 m/s = 54 km/h.

Example 5

A boat's downstream speed is 14 km/h and upstream speed is 8 km/h.

Solution: Stream speed = (14−8) ÷ 2 = 3 km/h.
