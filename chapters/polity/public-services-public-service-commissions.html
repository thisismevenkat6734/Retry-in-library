1. Introduction – పరిచయం

Public Services are the administrative services through which the government implements laws, policies and programmes. Public Service Commissions are constitutional bodies that conduct recruitment-related examinations and advise governments on specified service matters.

ప్రభుత్వ విధానాలు, చట్టాలు మరియు పథకాలను అమలు చేసే పరిపాలనా వ్యవస్థను ప్రభుత్వ సేవలు అంటారు. UPSC, State PSC వంటి రాజ్యాంగ సంస్థలు ప్రభుత్వ ఉద్యోగ నియామకాలు మరియు సేవా విషయాల్లో ముఖ్యమైన పాత్ర పోషిస్తాయి.

Constitutional provisions

  • Part XIV of the Constitution deals with Services under the Union and the States.
  • Articles 308–323 cover services and Public Service Commissions.
  • Articles 309–311 deal with recruitment, service conditions and safeguards for civil servants.
  • Articles 315–323 deal with Public Service Commissions.

Exam Point: Part XIV = Articles 308–323

3. Articles 309–313 – Services under Union and States

Article 309 – Recruitment and Conditions of Service

Subject to the Constitution, Parliament or the State Legislature may regulate recruitment and conditions of service of persons appointed to public services and posts in connection with the affairs of the Union or a State. Until appropriate legislation is made, the President or Governor may make rules, as applicable.

కేంద్రం లేదా రాష్ట్ర ప్రభుత్వ ఉద్యోగుల నియామకాలు, సేవా నిబంధనలను పార్లమెంట్ లేదా రాష్ట్ర శాసనసభ చట్టం ద్వారా నియంత్రించవచ్చు. సంబంధిత చట్టం వచ్చే వరకు రాష్ట్రపతి లేదా గవర్నర్ నియమాలు రూపొందించవచ్చు.

Article 310 – Doctrine of Pleasure

Members of defence services and civil services of the Union hold office during the pleasure of the President. Members of State civil services hold office during the pleasure of the Governor. This principle is subject to constitutional safeguards, particularly Article 311.

కేంద్ర ప్రభుత్వ సేవల్లో ఉన్నవారు రాష్ట్రపతి సంతోషం మేరకు, రాష్ట్ర ప్రభుత్వ సేవల్లో ఉన్నవారు గవర్నర్ సంతోషం మేరకు పదవిలో ఉంటారు. అయితే Article 311 వంటి రాజ్యాంగ రక్షణలు వర్తిస్తాయి.

Article 311 – Protection of Civil Servants

  • A civil servant cannot be dismissed or removed by an authority subordinate to the authority that appointed them.
  • No civil servant can be dismissed, removed or reduced in rank without an inquiry in which they are informed of the charges and given a reasonable opportunity of being heard.
  • The inquiry requirement may be dispensed with in constitutionally specified circumstances.

Exceptions to the inquiry requirement under Article 311(2)

  1. When dismissal, removal or reduction in rank is based on conduct that has led to conviction on a criminal charge.
  2. When the authority empowered to dismiss or remove is satisfied, for reasons recorded in writing, that holding an inquiry is not reasonably practicable.
  3. When the President or Governor, as applicable, is satisfied that holding an inquiry is not expedient in the interest of the security of the State.

Article 311 ఉద్యోగులకు అన్యాయమైన తొలగింపుల నుంచి రక్షణ ఇస్తుంది. అయితే రాజ్యాంగంలో పేర్కొన్న మూడు ప్రత్యేక పరిస్థితుల్లో విచారణను మినహాయించవచ్చు.

Article 312A – Certain Officers

Parliament has power to vary or revoke, by law, the conditions of service of certain persons appointed before the commencement of the Constitution, as provided in the Article.

Article 313 – Transitional Provisions

Existing service laws and rules continued after the commencement of the Constitution until altered or repealed by a competent authority, subject to the Constitution.

Article 314

Article 314, which formerly provided safeguards for certain officers of the former Secretary of State's services, was repealed by the 28th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1972.

4. Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)

The UPSC is a constitutional body established under Article 315. It conducts examinations for recruitment to various Union services and performs other functions specified by the Constitution and laws.

Composition and appointment – Article 316

  • The Chairman and other members are appointed by the President.
  • As nearly as may be, one-half of the members should be persons who have held office for at least ten years under the Government of India or a State Government.
  • The Constitution does not prescribe a fixed numerical strength for the Commission; it is determined by the President through regulations.

Term of office

  • UPSC Chairman and members hold office for six years from the date they enter office.
  • They must vacate office on attaining 65 years of age, whichever is earlier.
  • They are eligible for reappointment only as provided by the Constitution; a person who has held office as a member is not eligible for reappointment to that office, subject to the constitutional provisions for other PSC offices.

Removal – Article 317

The President may remove the Chairman or a member of the UPSC. In cases of misbehaviour, the President must refer the matter to the Supreme Court for an inquiry. If the Supreme Court reports that the person ought to be removed, the President may remove them.

Other specified grounds include insolvency, engaging in paid employment outside official duties, and infirmity of mind or body, as provided by the Constitution.

Suspension

The President may suspend the Chairman or a member of the UPSC while a reference for inquiry into misbehaviour is pending before the Supreme Court.

Functions – Article 320

  • Conducting examinations for appointments to Union services.
  • Advising on methods of recruitment.
  • Advising on principles to be followed in appointments, promotions and transfers.
  • Advising on disciplinary matters affecting civil servants.
  • Advising on claims for legal costs incurred by civil servants in proceedings connected with official duties.
  • Advising on claims for awards of injury pensions in specified circumstances.

UPSC కేంద్ర ప్రభుత్వ సేవలకు నియామక పరీక్షలు నిర్వహిస్తుంది. నియామకాలు, ప్రమోషన్లు, బదిలీలు, క్రమశిక్షణ చర్యలపై సలహాలు ఇస్తుంది.

Article 321 – Extension of Functions

Parliament may provide by law for additional functions to be performed by the UPSC or a State Public Service Commission in relation to services of local authorities or other public institutions.

Article 322 – Expenses

The expenses of the UPSC, including salaries and allowances of its members and staff, are charged on the Consolidated Fund of India.

Article 323 – Reports

The UPSC submits an annual report to the President. The President causes the report to be laid before each House of Parliament, along with a memorandum explaining cases where the advice of the Commission was not accepted.

5. State Public Service Commission (State PSC)

Each State may have its own Public Service Commission under Article 315.

Appointment

  • The Governor appoints the Chairman and members of a State PSC.
  • The term is six years or until attaining 62 years of age, whichever is earlier.
  • The Governor cannot independently remove a State PSC Chairman or member; removal is by the President under Article 317.

Functions

  • Conducts examinations for State services.
  • Advises the State Government on recruitment methods.
  • Advises on appointments, promotions and transfers.
  • Advises on disciplinary matters and specified service claims.

Annual Report

The State PSC submits its annual report to the Governor. The Governor lays it before the State Legislature, along with a memorandum explaining cases where the Commission's advice was not accepted.

రాష్ట్ర PSC తన వార్షిక నివేదికను గవర్నర్‌కు సమర్పిస్తుంది. గవర్నర్ దానిని రాష్ట్ర శాసనసభ ముందు ఉంచుతారు.

6. Joint State Public Service Commission (Joint PSC)

Under Article 315, two or more States may agree to have one Joint State Public Service Commission.

  • The State Legislatures concerned pass resolutions requesting its creation.
  • Parliament may establish the Joint PSC by law.
  • The Chairman and members are appointed by the President.
  • It serves the participating States according to the law establishing it.

రెండు లేదా అంతకంటే ఎక్కువ రాష్ట్రాలు ఒకే Joint PSC ఏర్పాటు చేసుకోవాలని తీర్మానిస్తే, పార్లమెంట్ చట్టం ద్వారా దానిని ఏర్పాటు చేయవచ్చు.

7. All India Services (AIS) – అఖిల భారత సర్వీసులు

All India Services are common to the Union and the States. Officers are recruited centrally but serve in both Union and State administrations.

Three All India Services

  1. Indian Administrative Service (IAS)
  2. Indian Police Service (IPS)
  3. Indian Forest Service (IFoS)

Important: Indian Forest Service was created in 1966 under the All India Services Act, 1951. It is distinct from the Indian Forest Service name sometimes used informally for State forest cadres.

Article 312 – Creation of All India Services

  • Rajya Sabha must pass a resolution declaring that it is necessary or expedient in the national interest to create an All India Service.
  • The resolution must be supported by not less than two-thirds of the members present and voting.
  • Parliament may then create the service by law.
  • Article 312 also provides for an All India Judicial Service, subject to its constitutional conditions and exclusions.

రాజ్యసభలో హాజరై ఓటు వేసిన సభ్యులలో కనీసం మూడింట రెండు వంతుల మద్దతుతో జాతీయ ప్రయోజనాల దృష్ట్యా అవసరమని తీర్మానం చేయాలి. తరువాత పార్లమెంట్ చట్టం ద్వారా All India Service ఏర్పాటు చేయవచ్చు.

All India Services Act, 1951

This Act provides the statutory framework for recruitment and conditions of service of members of All India Services.

8. Article 319 – Restrictions after Retirement

Article 319 places restrictions on holding further offices after a person ceases to hold office as Chairman or member of a Public Service Commission.

Former officeConstitutional eligibility
UPSC ChairmanNot eligible for further employment under the Government of India or a State Government.
State PSC ChairmanEligible to become UPSC Chairman or a member of UPSC, or Chairman of another State PSC, but not for other government employment.
UPSC memberEligible to become UPSC Chairman or a State PSC Chairman, but not for other government employment.
State PSC memberEligible to become UPSC Chairman or member, or Chairman of the same or another State PSC, but not for other government employment.

9. Independence of Public Service Commissions

  • Constitutional status protects the Commission's institutional position.
  • Members have a fixed constitutional term.
  • Removal is governed by Article 317.
  • Expenses are charged on the relevant Consolidated Fund under Article 322.
  • Restrictions on further employment are provided by Article 319.
  • Annual reporting and legislative scrutiny are provided under Article 323.

కమిషన్ల స్వతంత్రతను కాపాడేందుకు నియామకం, పదవీకాలం, తొలగింపు, ఖర్చులు మరియు పదవీ విరమణ తరువాత ఉద్యోగాలపై రాజ్యాంగ రక్షణలు కల్పించబడ్డాయి.
