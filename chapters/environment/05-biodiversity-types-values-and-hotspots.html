Biodiversity: Types, Values and Hotspots

Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Biodiversity includes genetic, species and ecosystem diversity. It supports food, medicine, pollination, soil fertility, water purification, climate regulation and cultural values. Threats include habitat loss, fragmentation, overexploitation, invasive alien species, pollution, climate change and illegal trade. Endemic species are naturally restricted to a region. IUCN Red List categories assess extinction risk. Hotspots have high endemism and severe habitat loss; India's hotspot regions include Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats-Sri Lanka and Sundaland (Nicobar Islands).
