Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Ecology studies interactions between organisms and their biotic and abiotic surroundings. Key levels are organism, population, community, ecosystem, biome and biosphere. Habitat is where an organism lives; niche is its functional role. Biotic factors include living organisms; abiotic factors include light, water, temperature, soil and minerals. Species interactions include competition, predation, parasitism, mutualism and commensalism. Population attributes include density, natality, mortality, age structure, immigration and emigration. Succession is community change over time; carrying capacity is the population an environment can support sustainably.
