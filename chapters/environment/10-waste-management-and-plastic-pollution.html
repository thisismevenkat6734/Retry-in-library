Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

The waste hierarchy prioritises prevention, reduction, reuse, recycling, recovery and safe disposal. Segregation at source separates wet, dry and domestic hazardous waste. India's Solid Waste Management Rules and Plastic Waste Management Rules were issued in 2016. Extended Producer Responsibility assigns prescribed post-consumer waste obligations to producers, importers and brand owners. Organic waste can be composted or biomethanated. E-waste and biomedical waste require specialised handling. Microplastics are plastic particles generally smaller than 5 mm.
