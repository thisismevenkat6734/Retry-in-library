Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Andhra Pradesh includes the Bay of Bengal coast, Eastern Ghats, river deltas, wetlands and mangroves. Coringa mangroves are associated with the Godavari estuary; Kolleru is a major freshwater lake between the Krishna and Godavari delta regions. Telangana lies largely on the Deccan Plateau and includes forest tracts, river valleys, tanks and reservoirs. Amrabad and Kawal are tiger reserves in Telangana. Shared concerns include water stress, habitat fragmentation, invasive species, waste and air pollution. Check current official state notifications for changing data and boundaries.
