Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Article 48A directs the State to protect and improve the environment; Article 51A(g) sets a citizen duty. Article 21 has been interpreted through case law to include environmental dimensions of life. Major laws include Water Act 1974, Air Act 1981, Environment Protection Act 1986, Wildlife Protection Act 1972 and Biological Diversity Act 2002. The NGT Act 2010 established the National Green Tribunal. MoEFCC, CPCB, SPCBs and NBA have important roles. Principles include precautionary principle and polluter pays.
