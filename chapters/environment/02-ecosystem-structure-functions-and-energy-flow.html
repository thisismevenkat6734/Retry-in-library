Ecosystem: Structure, Functions and Energy Flow

Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Ecosystems contain producers, consumers, decomposers and abiotic components. Producers convert inorganic materials into organic matter, usually using sunlight. Consumers occupy trophic levels; decomposers recycle nutrients from dead matter. GPP is total energy fixed by producers; NPP equals GPP minus producer respiration. Energy flow is unidirectional and dissipates as heat at each transfer. Nutrients cycle. Ecosystem services include provisioning, regulating, cultural and supporting benefits.
