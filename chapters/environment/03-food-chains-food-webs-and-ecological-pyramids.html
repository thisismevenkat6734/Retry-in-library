Food Chains, Food Webs and Ecological Pyramids

Chapter 3 of 15 · Telugu + English

← All Environment & Ecology Chapters

Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

A food chain is a linear feeding pathway; a food web connects multiple food chains. Grazing chains begin with living producers; detritus chains begin with dead organic matter. Producers occupy the first trophic level. Pyramids represent numbers, biomass or energy. The energy pyramid is always upright because energy is lost between levels. Bioaccumulation is build-up within an organism; biomagnification is increasing concentration at higher trophic levels. Keystone species have a disproportionately large ecological influence.
