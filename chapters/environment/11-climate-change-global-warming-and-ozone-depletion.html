Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Weather is short-term atmospheric condition; climate is long-term statistical pattern. Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit infrared radiation. Major gases include carbon dioxide, methane, nitrous oxide and fluorinated gases. Mitigation reduces emissions or enhances sinks; adaptation reduces vulnerability to impacts. Stratospheric ozone absorbs harmful UV radiation. The 1987 Montreal Protocol controls ozone-depleting substances. UNFCCC was adopted in 1992 and the Paris Agreement in 2015, aiming to hold warming well below 2°C and pursue 1.5°C.
