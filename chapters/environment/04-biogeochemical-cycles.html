Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Biogeochemical cycles move water and elements between organisms, atmosphere, hydrosphere and lithosphere. The water cycle includes evaporation, transpiration, condensation, precipitation, infiltration and runoff. Photosynthesis fixes carbon; respiration, decomposition and combustion release it. The nitrogen cycle includes fixation, nitrification, assimilation, ammonification and denitrification. The phosphorus cycle is mainly sedimentary and lacks a major gaseous phase. Excess nitrogen and phosphorus can cause eutrophication and oxygen depletion.

Key Terms / ముఖ్యమైన పదాలు

Evaporation

Liquid water changing into vapour

Transpiration

Water vapour loss from plants

Condensation

Water vapour cooling into liquid droplets

Photosynthesis

Fixation of carbon dioxide into organic matter

Nitrogen fixation

Conversion of atmospheric nitrogen into usable forms

Nitrification

Conversion of ammonium into nitrite and nitrate

Denitrification

Conversion of nitrate into gaseous nitrogen forms

Phosphorus cycle

Mainly sedimentary cycle without major gaseous phase

Eutrophication

Nutrient enrichment causing algal growth and oxygen loss

Carbon sink

Reservoir storing carbon
