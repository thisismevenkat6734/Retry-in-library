Environmental Pollution: Air, Water, Soil and Noise

Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Air pollutants include particulate matter, sulphur dioxide, nitrogen oxides, carbon monoxide, ozone and VOCs. PM2.5 can penetrate deep into the respiratory system. Photochemical smog forms through sunlight-driven reactions involving NOx and VOCs. Water pollution comes from sewage, industry, runoff, oil and plastics. BOD estimates oxygen used by microbes to decompose biodegradable matter; COD measures chemically oxidisable substances. Soil pollutants include heavy metals and pesticides. Noise is measured in decibels. CPCB and SPCBs monitor pollution.
