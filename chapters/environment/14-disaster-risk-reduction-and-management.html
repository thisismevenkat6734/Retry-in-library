Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

A hazard is a potentially damaging event; a disaster occurs when impacts overwhelm a community's coping capacity. Risk is shaped by hazard, exposure, vulnerability and capacity. The disaster cycle includes prevention, mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery. India's Disaster Management Act was enacted in 2005 and provides for NDMA, SDMAs and DDMAs. NDMA is chaired by the Prime Minister. Early warning, mock drills, resilient infrastructure and inclusive evacuation plans reduce risk.

Vulnerability

Conditions increasing susceptibility to harm

Exposure

People and assets located in hazard-prone areas

Mitigation

Measures reducing disaster risk or impact

Preparedness

Readiness to respond effectively

Disaster Management Act

Enacted in 2005

NDMA

NDRF

National Disaster Response Force

Sendai Framework

Global disaster-risk framework for 2015–2030

Early warning system
