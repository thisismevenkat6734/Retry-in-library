Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

Natural resources include renewable and non-renewable resources. Land degradation includes erosion, salinisation and desertification. Watersheds drain to a common outlet; rainwater harvesting supports storage and groundwater recharge. Forests protect watersheds, store carbon and conserve biodiversity. Sustainable development integrates environmental protection, social equity and economic well-being across generations. Circular economy promotes reduction, reuse, repair and recycling. Community participation improves management of common resources.

Key Terms / ముఖ్యమైన పదాలు

Renewable resource

Resource naturally replenished on a relevant timescale

Non-renewable resource

Resource formed too slowly to replace human use

Soil erosion

Removal of topsoil

Desertification

Land degradation in drylands

Watershed

Land draining to a common outlet

Rainwater harvesting

Collection or recharge of rainwater

Circular economy

System keeping materials in use

Intergenerational equity

Fairness between present and future generations

Carbon footprint

Emissions associated with an activity or entity

Groundwater recharge

Water replenishing an aquifer
