Biodiversity Conservation: In-situ and Ex-situ

Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

In-situ conservation protects species in natural habitats through national parks, sanctuaries, conservation reserves, community reserves and biosphere reserves. Ex-situ methods include zoos, botanical gardens, seed and gene banks, captive breeding and cryopreservation. Biosphere reserves commonly include core, buffer and transition zones. Project Tiger began in 1973; Project Elephant began in 1992. International frameworks include CBD, CITES, Ramsar and UNESCO's Man and the Biosphere Programme. Community participation and habitat corridors strengthen conservation.
