Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

India has tropical evergreen, semi-evergreen, deciduous, thorn, montane and littoral/swamp forests. The Forest Survey of India publishes the India State of Forest Report. The Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 provides a framework for wildlife protection and protected areas. Tiger reserves support tiger habitats and landscapes; elephant corridors connect habitats. Sacred groves and community conservation protect biodiversity beyond formal parks. Human-wildlife conflict can be reduced through corridor protection, early warning, safe infrastructure and community participation.
