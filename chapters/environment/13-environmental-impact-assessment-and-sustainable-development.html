Chapter Overview / అధ్యాయ పరిచయం

EIA identifies, predicts and evaluates likely environmental impacts before project decisions. Common stages include screening, scoping, baseline studies, impact prediction, alternatives, mitigation, public consultation, appraisal, decision and monitoring. An Environmental Management Plan specifies mitigation and monitoring responsibilities. Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising future generations. The UN 2030 Agenda has 17 Sustainable Development Goals, adopted in 2015. Planning should consider cumulative impacts and ecological carrying capacity.
