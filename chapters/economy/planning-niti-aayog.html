1. Meaning of Economic Planning | ఆర్థిక ప్రణాళిక అర్థం

English

Economic planning is a systematic process through which a government sets economic and social objectives, identifies priorities, allocates resources and implements programmes to achieve development goals within a specified period.

తెలుగు

ఒక నిర్దిష్ట కాలంలో ఆర్థిక, సామాజిక అభివృద్ధి లక్ష్యాలను సాధించడానికి ప్రభుత్వం ప్రాధాన్యతలను నిర్ణయించి, వనరులను కేటాయించి, కార్యక్రమాలను అమలు చేసే ప్రక్రియను Economic Planning అంటారు.

Objectives | లక్ష్యాలు

  • Economic Growth: Increase national income and productive capacity. / జాతీయ ఆదాయం, ఉత్పాదక సామర్థ్యాన్ని పెంచడం.
  • Employment: Generate productive employment opportunities. / ఉపాధి అవకాశాలు సృష్టించడం.
  • Poverty Reduction: Improve living standards and reduce poverty. / పేదరికాన్ని తగ్గించడం.
  • Balanced Regional Development: Reduce regional inequalities. / ప్రాంతీయ అసమానతలను తగ్గించడం.
  • Social Justice: Promote equitable distribution of opportunities and resources. / సామాజిక న్యాయాన్ని ప్రోత్సహించడం.
  • Self-Reliance: Strengthen domestic productive capacity. / స్వయం సమృద్ధిని పెంపొందించడం.
  • Modernisation: Promote technology, skills and productivity. / సాంకేతికత, నైపుణ్యాలు అభివృద్ధి చేయడం.

2. Economic Planning in India | భారతదేశంలో ఆర్థిక ప్రణాళిక

After independence, India adopted planned economic development to address poverty, low productivity, unemployment, weak industrialisation and regional disparities.

స్వాతంత్ర్యం తరువాత పేదరికం, నిరుద్యోగం, తక్కువ ఉత్పాదకత, పారిశ్రామిక వెనుకబాటు మరియు ప్రాంతీయ అసమానతలను తగ్గించేందుకు భారతదేశం ప్రణాళికాబద్ధమైన అభివృద్ధి మార్గాన్ని ఎంచుకుంది.

Important background

  • 1934 – Visvesvaraya Plan: M. Visvesvaraya proposed a planned approach to industrial development in his work Planned Economy for India.
  • 1938 – National Planning Committee: Established under the chairmanship of Jawaharlal Nehru by the Indian National Congress.
  • 1944 – Bombay Plan: A group of leading industrialists presented a plan for India's economic development.
  • 1944 – Gandhian Plan: Associated with decentralisation and village-based economic development, proposed by S.N. Agarwal.
  • 1945 – People's Plan: Associated with M.N. Roy and emphasis on agriculture and basic needs.
  • 1950 – Sarvodaya Plan: Associated with Jayaprakash Narayan and Gandhian principles.
  • 15 March 1950: Planning Commission was established by a Government of India resolution.
Exam Point: Planning Commission was established by an executive resolution, not by a constitutional provision or an Act of Parliament.

3. Planning Commission | ప్రణాళికా సంఘం

The Planning Commission was established on 15 March 1950 through a Government of India resolution. It was responsible for formulating Five-Year Plans and advising on development priorities and resource allocation.

15 మార్చి 1950న కేంద్ర ప్రభుత్వ తీర్మానం ద్వారా Planning Commission ఏర్పాటు చేయబడింది. ఐదేళ్ల ప్రణాళికల రూపకల్పన, అభివృద్ధి ప్రాధాన్యతలు మరియు వనరుల కేటాయింపులో ఇది కీలక పాత్ర పోషించింది.

Composition and leadership

  • Prime Minister served as the Chairperson.
  • A Deputy Chairperson was appointed by the government.
  • It included full-time members and other appointed members.
  • The Union Cabinet and ministries were involved in development policy and plan implementation.

Functions

  • Assess national resources.
  • Formulate Five-Year Plans.
  • Identify development priorities.
  • Recommend allocation of resources.
  • Review plan implementation and progress.
  • Advise on economic and social development policies.

End of Planning Commission

The Government of India discontinued the Planning Commission in 2014 and established NITI Aayog in January 2015.

2014లో Planning Commission ను రద్దు చేసి, 2015 జనవరిలో NITI Aayog ను ఏర్పాటు చేశారు.

4. Five-Year Plans in India | భారతదేశ ఐదేళ్ల ప్రణాళికలు

India followed a series of Five-Year Plans beginning in 1951. The plans reflected the changing priorities of the economy.

5. Important Five-Year Plan Features | ముఖ్య ప్రణాళికల ప్రత్యేకతలు

First Five-Year Plan (1951–1956)

  • Focused on agriculture, irrigation and community development.
  • Emphasised food production and rehabilitation after Partition.
  • Harrod-Domar model influenced its approach.
  • Bhakra-Nangal and other major multipurpose projects received attention.

Second Five-Year Plan (1956–1961)

  • Associated with P.C. Mahalanobis.
  • Focused on heavy industries and capital goods.
  • Expanded the public sector's role in industrial development.
  • Influenced by the Mahalanobis model.

Third Five-Year Plan (1961–1966)

  • Focused on self-reliance and self-sustaining growth.
  • Faced difficulties due to wars, droughts and economic pressures.
  • Was followed by three Annual Plans, commonly called the Plan Holiday.

Fourth Five-Year Plan (1969–1974)

  • Emphasised growth with stability.
  • Focused on reducing economic inequalities and self-reliance.

Fifth Five-Year Plan (1974–1979)

  • Emphasised poverty removal (Garibi Hatao) and self-reliance.
  • Minimum Needs Programme was an important feature.
  • Terminated early in 1978 by the Janata Government.

Sixth and Seventh Plans

  • Sixth Plan: Poverty reduction, employment and technological modernisation.
  • Seventh Plan: Food, work and productivity.

Eighth to Twelfth Plans

  • Eighth Plan: Human development and employment after economic reforms.
  • Ninth Plan: Growth with social justice and equality.
  • Tenth Plan: Faster growth and monitorable development targets.
  • Eleventh Plan: Faster and more inclusive growth.
  • Twelfth Plan: Faster, more inclusive and sustainable growth.

6. Plan Holiday and Rolling Plan | ప్రణాళిక విరామం, రోలింగ్ ప్లాన్

Plan Holiday (1966–1969)

After the Third Five-Year Plan, India adopted three Annual Plans instead of immediately starting the Fourth Plan. Economic difficulties, wars and droughts contributed to this change.

మూడవ ఐదేళ్ల ప్రణాళిక అనంతరం 1966–1969 మధ్య మూడు వార్షిక ప్రణాళికలు అమలు చేశారు. యుద్ధాలు, కరువులు మరియు ఆర్థిక సమస్యలు దీనికి కారణమయ్యాయి.

Rolling Plan

A Rolling Plan is revised periodically. Instead of having a fixed plan that remains unchanged for the entire period, targets and allocations are updated as circumstances change.

నిర్దిష్ట కాలానికి ఒకసారి ప్రణాళికను సవరించి, కొత్త పరిస్థితులకు అనుగుణంగా లక్ష్యాలు మరియు కేటాయింపులు మార్చే విధానాన్ని Rolling Plan అంటారు.

  • Introduced in India by the Janata Government for 1978–1980.
  • Associated with the government led by Morarji Desai.
  • The Sixth Five-Year Plan began in 1980 under the succeeding government.

7. NITI Aayog – Introduction | నీతి ఆయోగ్ పరిచయం

NITI Aayog stands for National Institution for Transforming India. It was established on 1 January 2015 through a Union Cabinet resolution as the Government of India's policy think tank.

NITI Aayog పూర్తి పేరు National Institution for Transforming India. 1 జనవరి 2015న కేంద్ర మంత్రివర్గ తీర్మానం ద్వారా దీనిని ఏర్పాటు చేశారు. ఇది ప్రభుత్వానికి విధాన రూపకల్పనలో సలహాలు అందించే సంస్థ.

Nature of NITI Aayog

  • Executive body established by a government resolution.
  • Not a constitutional body.
  • Not a statutory body established by an Act of Parliament.
  • Functions as a policy think tank.
  • Promotes cooperative and competitive federalism.
  • Encourages evidence-based policymaking and innovation.
Exam Point: NITI Aayog is neither a constitutional body nor a statutory body. It is an executive institution.

8. Objectives of NITI Aayog | నీతి ఆయోగ్ లక్ష్యాలు

  • Cooperative Federalism: Promote partnership between Union and States. / కేంద్ర, రాష్ట్రాల భాగస్వామ్యాన్ని పెంపొందించడం.
  • Competitive Federalism: Encourage States to improve performance through healthy competition. / రాష్ట్రాల మధ్య ఆరోగ్యకరమైన పోటీని ప్రోత్సహించడం.
  • Policy Innovation: Develop new policy ideas and solutions. / కొత్త విధానాలను రూపొందించడం.
  • Long-term Strategy: Prepare strategic and development policy frameworks. / దీర్ఘకాలిక అభివృద్ధి వ్యూహాలు రూపొందించడం.
  • Monitoring: Evaluate programmes and development outcomes. / ప్రభుత్వ కార్యక్రమాల ఫలితాలను అంచనా వేయడం.
  • Knowledge Sharing: Promote research, best practices and technology. / పరిశోధన, ఉత్తమ విధానాలను పంచుకోవడం.
  • Inclusive Development: Focus on disadvantaged groups and lagging regions. / వెనుకబడిన ప్రాంతాలు, వర్గాల అభివృద్ధిపై దృష్టి పెట్టడం.

9. Composition of NITI Aayog | నీతి ఆయోగ్ నిర్మాణం

The institutional structure includes the following positions and mechanisms under the applicable government resolution and subsequent changes.

  • Chairperson: Prime Minister of India.
  • Governing Council: Chief Ministers of States, Chief Ministers of Union Territories with legislatures, and Lieutenant Governors of other Union Territories.
  • Regional Councils: Formed for specific issues affecting more than one State or region.
  • Vice-Chairperson: Appointed by the Prime Minister.
  • Full-time Members: Experts and professionals appointed by the government.
  • Part-time Members: Selected from leading universities, research institutions and other organisations, as prescribed.
  • Ex-officio Members: Union Ministers nominated by the Prime Minister.
  • Special Invitees: Experts and specialists invited for particular purposes.
  • Chief Executive Officer (CEO): Appointed by the Prime Minister for a specified term.
NITI Aayog's composition may be modified by government orders. For current office-holders and the latest composition, check official NITI Aayog information.

10. Cooperative and Competitive Federalism | సహకార, పోటీ సమాఖ్యవాదం

Cooperative Federalism

Cooperative federalism means the Union and State Governments work together to formulate and implement policies.

కేంద్ర, రాష్ట్ర ప్రభుత్వాలు పరస్పర సహకారంతో విధానాలను రూపొందించి అమలు చేయడాన్ని Cooperative Federalism అంటారు.

  • Joint policy discussions.
  • Sharing of knowledge and resources.
  • Coordination on national development priorities.
  • Participation of States in national policy discussions.

Competitive Federalism

Competitive federalism encourages States and Union Territories to improve governance and development outcomes by comparing their performance and learning from better-performing regions.

రాష్ట్రాలు తమ పాలన, అభివృద్ధి ఫలితాలను మెరుగుపరచడానికి ఇతర రాష్ట్రాలతో ఆరోగ్యకరమైన పోటీ పడే విధానాన్ని Competitive Federalism అంటారు.

  • Performance rankings and indices.
  • Competition in health, education and infrastructure.
  • Sharing best practices.
  • Encouraging innovation and administrative improvements.

11. Important NITI Aayog Initiatives | ముఖ్య కార్యక్రమాలు

Aspirational Districts Programme (ADP)

Launched in January 2018, the programme aims to rapidly transform relatively underdeveloped districts through focused monitoring and competition.

2018 జనవరిలో ప్రారంభమైన ఈ కార్యక్రమం వెనుకబడిన జిల్లాల అభివృద్ధిని వేగవంతం చేయడం లక్ష్యంగా పెట్టుకుంది.

  • Focus on 112 aspirational districts.
  • Uses data-based monitoring.
  • Encourages district-level competition.
  • Focus areas include health and nutrition, education, agriculture and water resources, financial inclusion and skill development, and basic infrastructure.

Aspirational Blocks Programme

Launched in January 2023, it aims to improve development outcomes in selected blocks through focused monitoring and convergence of government schemes.

Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)

Promotes innovation and entrepreneurship through initiatives such as Atal Tinkering Labs and incubation support.

SDG India Index

Tracks the performance of States and Union Territories in relation to the Sustainable Development Goals.

National Monetisation Pipeline

Provides a framework for monetising selected public infrastructure assets through appropriate arrangements while retaining public ownership where applicable.

12. Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) | సుస్థిర అభివృద్ధి లక్ష్యాలు

The United Nations adopted the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development in 2015. It contains 17 Sustainable Development Goals and 169 targets.

2015లో ఐక్యరాజ్యసమితి 2030 సుస్థిర అభివృద్ధి అజెండాను ఆమోదించింది. ఇందులో 17 లక్ష్యాలు, 169 ఉపలక్ష్యాలు ఉన్నాయి.

17 SDGs

  1. No Poverty – పేదరిక నిర్మూలన.
  2. Zero Hunger – ఆకలి నిర్మూలన.
  3. Good Health and Well-being – ఆరోగ్యం, శ్రేయస్సు.
  4. Quality Education – నాణ్యమైన విద్య.
  5. Gender Equality – లింగ సమానత్వం.
  6. Clean Water and Sanitation – స్వచ్ఛమైన నీరు, పారిశుధ్యం.
  7. Affordable and Clean Energy – అందుబాటు ధరలో స్వచ్ఛమైన శక్తి.
  8. Decent Work and Economic Growth – ఉపాధి, ఆర్థిక వృద్ధి.
  9. Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure – పరిశ్రమలు, ఆవిష్కరణలు, మౌలిక సదుపాయాలు.
  10. Reduced Inequalities – అసమానతల తగ్గింపు.
  11. Sustainable Cities and Communities – సుస్థిర నగరాలు, సమాజాలు.
  12. Responsible Consumption and Production – బాధ్యతాయుత వినియోగం, ఉత్పత్తి.
  13. Climate Action – వాతావరణ చర్యలు.
  14. Life Below Water – సముద్ర జీవ సంరక్షణ.
  15. Life on Land – భూభాగ జీవ సంరక్షణ.
  16. Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions – శాంతి, న్యాయం, బలమైన సంస్థలు.
  17. Partnerships for the Goals – లక్ష్యాల కోసం భాగస్వామ్యాలు.

14. National Development Council (NDC) | జాతీయ అభివృద్ధి మండలి

The National Development Council was established on 6 August 1952. It was intended to strengthen and coordinate development planning between the Union and States.

6 ఆగస్టు 1952న National Development Council ఏర్పాటు చేయబడింది. కేంద్రం మరియు రాష్ట్రాల మధ్య అభివృద్ధి ప్రణాళికల సమన్వయానికి ఇది ఉపయోగపడింది.

  • Chaired by the Prime Minister.
  • Included Union Ministers, Chief Ministers of States and other members as prescribed.
  • Discussed national development priorities and plans.
  • Provided a platform for Centre-State coordination.
NDC was an executive body, not a constitutional body. It was associated with the Planning Commission era.

15. Limitations of Economic Planning | ఆర్థిక ప్రణాళిక పరిమితులు

  • Implementation Gaps: Plans may not achieve all targets. / లక్ష్యాల అమలులో లోపాలు.
  • Resource Constraints: Limited financial and human resources. / వనరుల కొరత.
  • Regional Inequality: Some regions may develop faster than others. / ప్రాంతీయ అసమానతలు.
  • Administrative Delays: Delays can affect project execution. / పరిపాలనా జాప్యాలు.
  • Data Limitations: Poor or delayed data can affect planning. / గణాంకాల లోపాలు.
  • Changing Conditions: Global shocks and domestic changes may require revision. / మారుతున్న పరిస్థితులకు అనుగుణంగా ప్రణాళిక సవరణ అవసరం.
