1. Introduction to Indian Agriculture | భారత వ్యవసాయ పరిచయం

English

Agriculture is one of the important sectors of the Indian economy. It provides food, raw materials for industries, employment and livelihood to a large section of the population. It also supports allied activities such as dairy, fisheries, poultry and animal husbandry.

తెలుగు

భారత ఆర్థిక వ్యవస్థలో వ్యవసాయం అత్యంత ముఖ్యమైన రంగం. ఇది ఆహారం, పరిశ్రమలకు ముడిసరుకు, ఉపాధి మరియు జీవనోపాధిని అందిస్తుంది. పాడి పరిశ్రమ, చేపల పెంపకం, కోళ్ల పెంపకం, పశుపోషణ వంటి అనుబంధ రంగాలకు కూడా వ్యవసాయం ఆధారం.

Importance | ప్రాముఖ్యత

  • Food security / ఆహార భద్రత.
  • Employment and livelihoods / ఉపాధి, జీవనోపాధి.
  • Raw materials for industries / పరిశ్రమలకు ముడిసరుకు.
  • Rural development / గ్రామీణ అభివృద్ధి.
  • Contribution to exports / ఎగుమతులకు సహకారం.
  • Demand for industrial goods / పారిశ్రామిక వస్తువులకు డిమాండ్.
Agriculture's contribution to GDP and employment changes over time. Do not assume that its employment share and GDP share are equal.

2. Types of Agriculture in India | భారతదేశ వ్యవసాయ రకాలు

Subsistence Farming – జీవనాధార వ్యవసాయం

Farming mainly undertaken to meet the food requirements of the farmer's household, often on small holdings.

రైతు కుటుంబ అవసరాల కోసం ప్రధానంగా చేసే వ్యవసాయం.

Commercial Farming – వాణిజ్య వ్యవసాయం

Production is mainly intended for sale in the market.

మార్కెట్లో విక్రయించి ఆదాయం పొందేందుకు చేసే వ్యవసాయం.

Plantation Agriculture – తోటల వ్యవసాయం

Large-scale cultivation of a single commercial crop, often using hired labour and organised processing.

పెద్ద స్థాయిలో ఒక వాణిజ్య పంటను సాగు చేయడం. ఉదాహరణలు: టీ, కాఫీ, రబ్బరు.

Intensive Farming – సాంద్ర వ్యవసాయం

High inputs of labour, capital and other resources are used on relatively small areas of land.

Extensive Farming – విస్తృత వ్యవసాయం

Farming over large areas with relatively lower inputs per unit of land.

Mixed Farming – మిశ్రమ వ్యవసాయం

Crop cultivation is combined with livestock rearing.

పంటల సాగుతో పాటు పశుపోషణ చేయడం.

Shifting Cultivation – పోడు వ్యవసాయం

A traditional system in which land is cultivated for a period and then left fallow while cultivation shifts elsewhere.

కొంతకాలం ఒక భూమిలో సాగు చేసి, తరువాత మరో ప్రాంతానికి సాగును మార్చే సంప్రదాయ పద్ధతి.

3. Cropping Seasons in India | పంటల కాలాలు

Kharif Crops – ఖరీఫ్ పంటలు

Generally sown with the onset of the southwest monsoon and harvested around September–October or later, depending on the crop and region.

  • Rice / వరి.
  • Maize / మొక్కజొన్న.
  • Cotton / పత్తి.
  • Groundnut / వేరుశెనగ.
  • Soybean / సోయాబీన్.
  • Bajra / సజ్జలు.

Rabi Crops – రబీ పంటలు

Generally sown during the winter season and harvested in spring.

  • Wheat / గోధుమ.
  • Barley / బార్లీ.
  • Mustard / ఆవాలు.
  • Gram / శనగలు.
  • Peas / బఠానీలు.

Zaid Crops – జైద్ పంటలు

Short-duration crops grown mainly between the Rabi and Kharif seasons.

  • Watermelon / పుచ్చకాయ.
  • Muskmelon / కర్బూజ.
  • Cucumber / దోసకాయ.
  • Some seasonal vegetables.
Exam Point: Kharif = Monsoon; Rabi = Winter; Zaid = Summer/inter-seasonal period.

5. Land Reforms in India | భూ సంస్కరణలు

Land reforms refer to changes in land ownership, tenancy arrangements and landholding structures to improve equity, productivity and rural development.

భూమి యాజమాన్యం, కౌలు వ్యవస్థ మరియు భూమి పంపిణీలో సమానత్వం, ఉత్పాదకత పెంచేందుకు చేపట్టే మార్పులను భూ సంస్కరణలు అంటారు.

Major components

  • Abolition of Intermediaries: Removal of zamindars and other intermediaries between cultivators and the State.
  • Tenancy Reforms: Regulation of rent, security of tenure and protection of tenant rights.
  • Land Ceiling: Limits on the maximum landholding permitted under State laws, with surplus land potentially redistributed.
  • Consolidation of Holdings: Combining fragmented plots into more manageable holdings.
  • Land Records Modernisation: Improving ownership records, maps and digital land administration.

Challenges

  • Unequal distribution of land.
  • Fragmentation of holdings.
  • Incomplete land records.
  • Disputes and lengthy legal processes.
  • Uneven implementation across States.

6. Green Revolution | హరిత విప్లవం

The Green Revolution refers to the increase in agricultural production associated with high-yielding varieties, irrigation, fertilisers, pesticides and improved farming practices.

అధిక దిగుబడి విత్తనాలు, నీటిపారుదల, ఎరువులు, పురుగుమందులు మరియు ఆధునిక సాగు పద్ధతుల వల్ల వ్యవసాయ ఉత్పత్తి పెరగడాన్ని హరిత విప్లవం అంటారు.

Important features

  • High-yielding variety (HYV) seeds.
  • Expansion of irrigation facilities.
  • Use of chemical fertilisers and pesticides.
  • Mechanisation and improved farming practices.
  • Greater use of institutional credit and agricultural research.

Important personalities

  • Norman Borlaug: Internationally recognised for contributions to the Green Revolution in agriculture.
  • M.S. Swaminathan: Widely known as the Father of the Green Revolution in India.

Benefits

  • Higher foodgrain production.
  • Improved food security.
  • Reduced dependence on food imports in later years.
  • Development of agricultural infrastructure in major adopting regions.

Limitations

  • Regional inequalities in benefits.
  • Excessive groundwater extraction in some areas.
  • Soil degradation and chemical-related concerns.
  • Greater benefits initially concentrated in wheat and rice.

7. White, Blue, Yellow and Other Revolutions | ఇతర వ్యవసాయ విప్లవాలు

  • Green Revolution: Foodgrain production / ఆహార ధాన్యాలు.
  • White Revolution: Milk production / పాల ఉత్పత్తి.
  • Blue Revolution: Fisheries and aquaculture / మత్స్య రంగం.
  • Yellow Revolution: Oilseed production / నూనెగింజలు.
  • Golden Revolution: Horticulture and honey production are commonly associated with this term.
  • Silver Revolution: Egg and poultry production.
  • Red Revolution: Often associated with meat or tomato production in different exam references; context matters.
White Revolution: Operation Flood was a major dairy development programme led by the National Dairy Development Board (NDDB), associated with Verghese Kurien.

8. Agricultural Irrigation | వ్యవసాయ నీటిపారుదల

Irrigation is the artificial supply of water to agricultural land to support crop growth when rainfall is insufficient or irregular.

వర్షపాతం సరిపోని సందర్భాల్లో పంటల పెరుగుదలకు అవసరమైన నీటిని కృత్రిమంగా అందించడాన్ని నీటిపారుదల అంటారు.

Major methods

  • Canal Irrigation: Water supplied through canals from rivers or reservoirs.
  • Well Irrigation: Water drawn from open wells.
  • Tube-well Irrigation: Groundwater extracted using tube wells and pumps.
  • Tank Irrigation: Water stored in tanks and reservoirs.
  • Drip Irrigation: Water delivered near plant roots through pipes and emitters.
  • Sprinkler Irrigation: Water sprayed over crops in a manner similar to rainfall.

Micro-irrigation

Drip and sprinkler systems can improve water-use efficiency when properly designed and managed.

Challenges

  • Groundwater depletion.
  • Unequal access to irrigation.
  • Waterlogging and salinity in some irrigated areas.
  • High initial investment in modern systems.

9. Minimum Support Price (MSP) | కనీస మద్దతు ధర

Minimum Support Price is an announced price intended to provide a price-support mechanism to farmers for specified crops. The Government announces MSPs for 22 mandated crops and a Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) for sugarcane.

నిర్దిష్ట పంటలకు రైతులకు ధరల మద్దతు అందించేందుకు ప్రభుత్వం ప్రకటించే ధరను Minimum Support Price అంటారు. 22 నిర్దేశిత పంటలకు MSP ప్రకటిస్తారు. చెరకు కోసం FRP ప్రకటిస్తారు.

Important facts

  • MSP recommendations are made by the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP).
  • The Government takes the final decision on MSP.
  • MSP is announced before the relevant sowing seasons.
  • MSP is intended to protect farmers from sharp price declines.
  • Procurement is not guaranteed for every crop, every farmer or every region at unlimited quantities.

22 mandated MSP crops

  • 7 cereals: Paddy, wheat, maize, jowar, bajra, ragi and barley.
  • 5 pulses: Gram, tur, moong, urad and lentil.
  • 7 oilseeds: Groundnut, rapeseed-mustard, soybean, sunflower seed, sesamum, safflower and nigerseed.
  • 3 commercial crops: Cotton, jute and copra.
Sugarcane receives Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP), which is distinct from MSP.

10. Agricultural Credit | వ్యవసాయ రుణాలు

Agricultural credit provides farmers with funds for seeds, fertilisers, machinery, irrigation, livestock and other farming activities.

విత్తనాలు, ఎరువులు, యంత్రాలు, నీటిపారుదల మరియు పశుపోషణ కోసం రైతులకు అందించే రుణాలను వ్యవసాయ రుణాలు అంటారు.

Sources of credit

  • Institutional: Commercial banks, Regional Rural Banks, Cooperative banks and other eligible institutions.
  • Non-institutional: Moneylenders, traders and informal lenders.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

The Kisan Credit Card scheme provides timely and flexible credit to eligible farmers for agricultural and allied activities, subject to scheme guidelines.

  • Introduced in 1998.
  • Helps meet cultivation expenses.
  • Can cover eligible allied activities and working capital requirements.
  • Credit availability depends on eligibility and bank assessment.

NABARD

The National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development was established on 12 July 1982. It supports agriculture and rural development through refinance, development initiatives and institutional support.

11. Agricultural Insurance | పంటల బీమా

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

PMFBY was launched in 2016 to provide crop insurance support against specified crop losses arising from notified risks and natural calamities, subject to scheme conditions.

2016లో ప్రారంభమైన PMFBY పథకం ప్రకృతి వైపరీత్యాలు మరియు నిర్దేశిత ప్రమాదాల వల్ల పంట నష్టపోయినప్పుడు అర్హత కలిగిన రైతులకు బీమా రక్షణ కల్పిస్తుంది.

  • Launched in 2016.
  • Provides insurance cover for notified crops and areas.
  • Includes specified risks such as prevented sowing and post-harvest losses, subject to conditions.
  • Farmer premium rates under the scheme have been specified for different crop seasons and categories.
  • Claims depend on notified risks, assessment and scheme rules.

12. Important Agricultural Schemes | ముఖ్య వ్యవసాయ పథకాలు

PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi provides income support to eligible landholding farmer families, subject to exclusion rules.

  • Launched in 2019.
  • ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments, subject to eligibility.
  • Direct Benefit Transfer is used.

Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

Aims to improve irrigation coverage and water-use efficiency. Its guiding slogan is “Har Khet Ko Pani” and “More Crop Per Drop”.

Soil Health Card Scheme

Provides information on soil nutrient status and recommendations for balanced fertiliser use.

e-NAM

National Agriculture Market is an electronic trading platform intended to integrate agricultural markets and improve market access.

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana

Promotes organic farming through cluster-based approaches and related support.

Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana

Supports agricultural development strategies and investment by States under applicable guidelines.

PM-AASHA

Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan aims to provide price support to farmers through specified mechanisms.

13. Food Security and Public Distribution System | ఆహార భద్రత

Food security means ensuring that people have physical and economic access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food.

ప్రజలకు సరిపడా, సురక్షితమైన, పోషకాహారాన్ని అందుబాటులో ఉంచడాన్ని ఆహార భద్రత అంటారు.

Public Distribution System (PDS)

  • Distributes essential food commodities through Fair Price Shops.
  • Supports eligible households under applicable policies.
  • Plays a role in food security and price stabilisation.

National Food Security Act, 2013

  • Provides a legal framework for subsidised foodgrain entitlements to eligible persons.
  • Covers up to 75% of the rural population and up to 50% of the urban population under the Act.
  • Includes Priority Households and Antyodaya Anna Yojana households.
  • Supports nutritional security for women and children through specified provisions.

Buffer Stock

Foodgrain stocks maintained by the government to meet food security needs, support distribution and manage supply fluctuations.

Food Corporation of India (FCI)

Established in 1965, FCI plays an important role in procurement, storage, movement and distribution of foodgrains under government policies.

14. Agricultural Marketing | వ్యవసాయ మార్కెటింగ్

Agricultural marketing involves the processes through which farm produce is assembled, graded, stored, transported, processed and sold.

వ్యవసాయ ఉత్పత్తులను సేకరించడం, నాణ్యత వర్గీకరణ, నిల్వ, రవాణా, ప్రాసెసింగ్ మరియు విక్రయించే ప్రక్రియను వ్యవసాయ మార్కెటింగ్ అంటారు.

Important concepts

  • APMC: Agricultural Produce Market Committees regulate notified agricultural markets under State laws.
  • e-NAM: Electronic platform connecting participating agricultural markets.
  • Warehousing: Helps reduce post-harvest losses and supports storage.
  • Cold Chain: Temperature-controlled storage and transport for perishable goods.
  • Grading: Classification based on quality and standards.

Challenges

  • Market intermediaries and fragmented supply chains.
  • Inadequate storage and cold-chain facilities.
  • Price fluctuations.
  • Limited market information for farmers.
  • High transportation costs.

15. Rural Economy and Rural Development | గ్రామీణ ఆర్థిక వ్యవస్థ

The rural economy includes agriculture, allied activities, rural industries, services, trade and employment in villages and smaller settlements.

గ్రామీణ ప్రాంతాల్లో వ్యవసాయం, పశుపోషణ, చిన్న పరిశ్రమలు, సేవలు, వ్యాపారం మరియు ఉపాధి కార్యకలాపాలన్నీ గ్రామీణ ఆర్థిక వ్యవస్థలో భాగం.

Major components

  • Agriculture and horticulture.
  • Dairy, poultry and fisheries.
  • Handloom and handicrafts.
  • Rural transport and retail trade.
  • Small and micro enterprises.
  • Education, healthcare and other services.

Challenges

  • Seasonal and disguised unemployment.
  • Low productivity.
  • Limited access to institutional credit.
  • Infrastructure gaps.
  • Rural poverty and migration.
  • Limited access to quality education and healthcare in some regions.

16. Rural Employment and Livelihood Schemes | గ్రామీణ ఉపాధి పథకాలు

Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)

MGNREGA, enacted in 2005, provides a legal guarantee of at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work, subject to the Act.

2005లో రూపొందిన MGNREGA చట్టం ప్రకారం, గ్రామీణ కుటుంబంలోని వయోజన సభ్యులు నైపుణ్యం అవసరం లేని శారీరక పనికి సిద్ధంగా ఉంటే, ఆ కుటుంబానికి ఆర్థిక సంవత్సరంలో కనీసం 100 రోజుల వేతన ఉపాధి హామీ ఉంటుంది.

  • Demand-driven wage employment programme.
  • At least 100 days of guaranteed employment per rural household per financial year under the Act.
  • Social audit is an important accountability mechanism.
  • Gram Sabha has an important role in social audit.
  • Promotes creation of rural assets.

Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – NRLM

Promotes rural livelihoods through Self-Help Groups, financial inclusion and livelihood support.

Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY)

Aims to provide rural road connectivity to eligible habitations under programme guidelines.

National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)

Provides social assistance to eligible vulnerable groups through specified pension and assistance components.

17. Self-Help Groups (SHGs) | స్వయం సహాయక సంఘాలు

Self-Help Groups are small voluntary groups, often consisting of people with common socio-economic interests, who save regularly and may access credit collectively.

సాధారణంగా ఒకే విధమైన సామాజిక, ఆర్థిక పరిస్థితులు కలిగిన వ్యక్తులు కలిసి పొదుపు చేసి, అవసరమైనప్పుడు సమూహంగా రుణాలు పొందే సంఘాలను SHGs అంటారు.

Importance

  • Promote savings habits.
  • Improve access to credit.
  • Support women’s economic empowerment.
  • Encourage micro-enterprises.
  • Strengthen community participation.

SHG-Bank Linkage Programme

NABARD played an important role in promoting the SHG-Bank Linkage Programme, which connects eligible Self-Help Groups with formal banking services.

18. Allied Agricultural Activities | వ్యవసాయ అనుబంధ రంగాలు

Dairy Farming

Milk production and related activities provide regular income and support rural employment.

Fisheries

Includes inland fisheries, marine fisheries, aquaculture and fish processing.

Poultry

Includes rearing birds for eggs and meat.

Animal Husbandry

Includes management and breeding of livestock such as cattle, buffaloes, sheep and goats.

Horticulture

Includes fruits, vegetables, flowers, spices and plantation crops.

Diversification into allied activities can reduce dependence on a single crop and create additional income opportunities for rural households.

19. Agriculture in Andhra Pradesh | ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్ వ్యవసాయం

Andhra Pradesh has diverse agro-climatic conditions and an important agricultural and fisheries economy.

Important features

  • Major crops include paddy, maize, cotton, chilli, tobacco, groundnut, pulses and horticultural crops.
  • Godavari and Krishna river systems support irrigation in important agricultural regions.
  • Coastal districts have significant aquaculture and marine fisheries activity.
  • Horticulture includes mango, banana, citrus, oil palm and other crops.
  • Drip and sprinkler irrigation are relevant in water-stressed areas.
  • Agricultural marketing, cold storage and food processing are important development areas.

Major agricultural issues

  • Dependence on monsoon in some areas.
  • Groundwater management.
  • Crop price volatility.
  • Floods and cyclones in coastal areas.
  • Storage and post-harvest losses.

20. Sustainable Agriculture | సుస్థిర వ్యవసాయం

Sustainable agriculture aims to produce sufficient food while conserving soil, water, biodiversity and other natural resources for future generations.

Methods

  • Crop rotation.
  • Organic manure and compost.
  • Integrated Pest Management (IPM).
  • Water-efficient irrigation.
  • Agroforestry.
  • Soil conservation and reduced erosion.
  • Climate-resilient crop varieties.

Climate-smart agriculture

Climate-smart agriculture aims to improve productivity, strengthen resilience to climate change and reduce or remove greenhouse gas emissions where possible.
