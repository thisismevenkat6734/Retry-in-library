Information to Include in a Report

When a public contact channel becomes available, including the following details can help us investigate your request:

  • The page URL or page title.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • The exact incorrect statement, if reporting a factual error.
  • A reliable source supporting the correction.
  • For technical problems, the device and browser details, if relevant.

Do not send passwords, banking details, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Response Expectations

We aim to review genuine reports and suggestions. However, a response time or resolution deadline is not guaranteed.

Submitting a correction or copyright concern does not automatically mean the content will be changed or removed. Each request may require verification.
