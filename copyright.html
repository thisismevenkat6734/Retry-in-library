Copyright Policy

Information about original content, third-party materials, permitted use and copyright concerns.

Important

RETRY IN LIBRARY aims to publish original educational explanations and practice material. Third-party content is not automatically free to copy or redistribute.

1. Original Website Content

Unless otherwise stated, original text, explanations, study notes, question explanations, website design, graphics and other materials created for RETRY IN LIBRARY are protected by applicable intellectual property laws.

The website does not claim ownership of third-party materials that may be referenced or linked.

2. Permitted Personal Use

Visitors may access the website for personal learning and non-commercial examination preparation.

Unless a separate licence or written permission allows it, users must not:

  • Republish substantial portions of original website content.
  • Sell, rent or commercially redistribute our original notes.
  • Copy website content into another paid educational product.
  • Remove copyright notices or attribution.
  • Present our original material as their own work.
  • Use automated tools to systematically copy or extract website content in a way that violates applicable law or site terms.

3. Government and Public Information

The website may refer to government notifications, public reports, examination syllabi, laws, policies, statistics and other official information.

The copyright status and reuse conditions of such material depend on the original source and applicable licence or law.

A document being publicly accessible does not automatically mean that it can be reproduced without restrictions.

4. Textbooks, Newspapers and Commercial Publications

We do not intend to reproduce unauthorised scans, complete commercial textbooks, paid coaching materials, copyrighted question banks or substantial portions of newspaper articles.

Where appropriate, educational topics may be explained independently in our own words, with references to reliable sources.

Users should obtain books and paid educational resources through authorised channels.

5. Previous-Year Questions

Examination questions may be subject to copyright, licensing conditions or other reuse restrictions.

We will publish previous-year questions only when we have a reasonable basis to believe that the material may lawfully be used in the intended manner.

Questions written independently for practice will be identified as original practice questions and will not be falsely labelled as official previous-year questions.

6. Third-Party Names, Logos and Trademarks

Names, logos, trademarks and brand identifiers belonging to examination authorities, government bodies, publishers and other organisations remain the property of their respective owners.

Their appearance on this website is for identification or informational reference where appropriate.

It does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership or official affiliation.

7. External Links and Licences

We may link to official websites, open educational resources and materials made available under specific licences.

Users must follow the terms and attribution requirements specified by the original publisher or licence.

We do not grant permission to reuse third-party content simply by linking to it.

8. Reporting Copyright Concerns

If you believe that content published on RETRY IN LIBRARY infringes your copyright or other intellectual property rights, please submit a detailed report through the available contact channel once it is published.

A useful report should include:

  • Your name and a reliable contact method.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work.
  • The exact page URL and location of the disputed content.
  • An explanation of your ownership or authority to report.
  • Supporting evidence or relevant source links.
  • A statement that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Do not submit passwords, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

9. Review and Content Removal

We will review sufficiently detailed reports and may temporarily restrict access to disputed material while investigating a concern.

Depending on the findings, we may correct, replace, attribute or remove the material where appropriate.

Submitting a report does not automatically establish infringement or guarantee removal.

10. Corrections and Content Removal

For general factual errors, outdated material or other content concerns, visit our Corrections & Content Removal page.

11. Policy Updates

This policy may be revised as the website's content, services and applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
