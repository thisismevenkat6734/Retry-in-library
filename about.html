Who We Are

RETRY IN LIBRARY is an independent digital educational resource intended to support students and competitive examination aspirants in India.

Our goal is to organise study topics, practice questions, examination resources and revision material in one accessible learning environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make organised examination preparation resources easier to access, understand and revise.

  • Provide structured subject-wise learning resources.
  • Support Telugu and English learning wherever content is available in both languages.
  • Offer original practice questions and mock tests.
  • Organise current affairs with dates and source references.
  • Help learners track available practice activity locally.
  • Encourage independent learning and regular revision.

Who Can Use This Library?

The platform is being developed to support aspirants preparing for a broad range of examinations.

State-Level Examinations

APPSC, TGPSC, state police recruitment, teacher recruitment and other state-level examinations.

National-Level Examinations

UPSC, SSC, Railway, Banking and other central recruitment examinations.

Subject Preparation

History, Polity, Economy, Geography, Science, Mathematics, Reasoning, Languages and Computer Knowledge.

State-Specific Learning

Andhra Pradesh and Telangana history, geography, economy, culture and administration.

Our Learning Approach

  1. Understand: Learn concepts through organised subject and chapter resources.
  2. Practise: Attempt original multiple-choice questions.
  3. Evaluate: Use timed mock tests to review your performance.
  4. Revise: Revisit topics and strengthen weaker areas.
  5. Stay updated: Study current affairs with source and date references.

Telugu & English Learning

We aim to make learning resources accessible to Telugu-speaking aspirants as well as learners who prefer English.

Bilingual explanations will be added progressively. Not every existing page or resource is currently available in both languages.

Privacy & Technology

The platform is designed around a static website architecture. Certain practice statistics and preferences may be stored in your browser using LocalStorage.

  • No user account is required for the currently implemented learning pages.
  • Local progress data is not automatically synced between devices.
  • Clearing browser storage may remove saved progress.
  • Offline access depends on the pages and resources already cached or downloaded.

Read our Privacy Policy for further information about the site's data practices.

Content Quality & Accuracy

Educational content should be accurate, understandable and appropriately sourced. Current-affairs entries and examination details may change over time.

We aim to identify source material, correct reported errors and distinguish original practice questions from verified previous-year questions.

If you identify an error, please use our Correction & Content Removal page.

Independent Educational Platform

RETRY IN LIBRARY is not an official website of APPSC, TGPSC, UPSC, SSC, Railway Recruitment Boards, banking recruitment bodies or any government department.

Examination notifications, eligibility, syllabus, dates and selection rules must always be checked through the relevant official authority.

Use of an examination authority's name is only to identify the examination for educational reference.
