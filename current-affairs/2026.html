Standard News Entry Format

Every published current-affairs item should follow this structure.

News headline
  • Event date: Exact date of the event or announcement.
  • Category: National / International / AP / Telangana / Economy / Science / Environment / Sports.
  • Summary: Original, concise explanation of the event.
  • Why important: Relevance to competitive examinations.
  • Key facts: Important names, dates, figures and institutions, where verified.
  • Source: Direct source link or official publication reference.
  • Published on: Date this entry was added to the library.
  • Last verified: Date the facts and source were checked.

Reliable Information Sources

Prefer official primary sources. Use reputable news organisations for context, and verify important claims against original documents whenever possible.

  • Government of India: Press Information Bureau
  • Parliament: Parliament of India
  • RBI: Reserve Bank of India
  • ISRO: Indian Space Research Organisation
  • Election Commission: Election Commission of India
  • Andhra Pradesh Government: Official AP Government Portal
  • Telangana Government: Official Telangana Government Portal
  • United Nations: United Nations
  • World Bank: World Bank
  • IMF: International Monetary Fund

How to Study Current Affairs

  1. Read important events month by month.
  2. Understand why each event matters.
  3. Connect news with History, Polity, Economy, Geography and Science.
  4. Revise important names, dates, institutions and reports.
  5. Practise MCQs after studying each topic.
  6. Recheck changing facts before the examination.
